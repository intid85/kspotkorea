🇰🇷 Essential Korea Travel Survival Tip

Why Google Maps Walking Directions Don't Work in South Korea

Due to South Korean geospatial regulations, Google Maps cannot provide walking or driving turn-by-turn routes inside Korea. Searching English drama location names inside Korean map apps also often returns zero results. K-Spot Korea (kspotkorea.co.kr) solves this: click Naver Map or KakaoMap on any card to jump straight to the exact Korean place pin, or tap Copy Korean Address to show your taxi driver.
