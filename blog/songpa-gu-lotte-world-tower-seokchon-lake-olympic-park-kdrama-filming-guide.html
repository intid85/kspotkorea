Why Songpa-gu Is a K-Drama and K-Pop Playground

Songpa-gu sits on the southeast side of the Han River in Seoul and is home to Lotte World Tower, Seokchon Lake and Olympic Park. Korea's public KCISA filming-location dataset lists 203 records with a Songpa address: 89 from dramas, 78 from variety and food shows, 33 from idol and artist visits and 3 from films. Lotte World alone accounts for about 32 of those entries, and Olympic Park for about 13.

That mix makes Songpa an easy first-timer district. Everything below is reachable by subway and fits one half-day. Each stop lists the exact Korean address for Naver Map, KakaoMap or a taxi driver. The dataset records where scenes were set, so treat these as atmosphere stops rather than exact set recreations.

The easiest base is Jamsil Station, where Seoul Subway Line 2 and Line 8 meet. From there Lotte World, Lotte World Mall and Seokchon Lake are all within a short walk, and Olympic Park is a few stops away on Line 8 or Line 5.

Lotte World and Lotte World Tower

Lotte World (롯데월드)

Lotte World (서울특별시 송파구 올림픽로 240) is the most-listed place in Songpa, and the dataset ties it to a long run of romance scenes. The carousel and rides appear in Stairway to Heaven, The Greatest Love, Athena: Goddess of War, Alice, Familiar Wife and Our Beloved Summer. The indoor ice rink is another recurring location: True Beauty, Mental Coach Jegal and Clean with Passion for Now all list skating scenes there. Idol content also shows up, including variety shoots with CIX and Pentagon.

Take Line 2 or Line 8 to Jamsil Station and follow the underground signs to Lotte World, which is directly connected. Hours change by season, so check ticket options before you go.

Lotte World Tower and Lotte World Mall (롯데월드타워·롯데월드몰)

Lotte World Tower and Lotte World Mall share the address 서울특별시 송파구 올림픽로 300. The dataset links the complex to Goblin, where Kim Shin and Ji Eun-tak are seen shopping for household goods at a store on the fourth floor, as well as Tomorrow, Hotel Del Luna, Melting Me Softly, Life and Search: WWW. The Aquarium in the mall basement appears in Search: WWW, and the Lotte Concert Hall on the eighth floor is listed for Sisyphus: The Myth and Thirty-Nine. The tower's Signiel hotel and its sky bar are listed for Encounter.

Jamsil Station Exit 1 and 2 connect through the underground concourse to the mall.

Seokchon Lake: Cherry Blossoms and Night Walks

Seokchon Lake (석촌호수, 서울특별시 송파구 잠실동 47) wraps around the base of Lotte World and is split into an east lake and a west lake by a causeway. The KCISA data lists The Greatest Love, where Dok-go Jin confesses under the cherry blossoms, and Love Alarm, where Kim So-hyun and Song Kang take a night walk by the lake. Variety shows and idols also appear, including Twice members on a food-show walk and a lakeside spot RM shared online.

The loop path is flat, so it works as a gentle walk between the Lotte World side and the Songridan-gil side. Cherry blossoms usually peak in early April and attract crowds, while autumn colors are quieter. Seokchon Station (Lines 8 and 9) and Jamsil Station both sit close to the shore.

Olympic Park and the SOMA Museum

Olympic Park (올림픽공원)

Olympic Park (서울특별시 송파구 올림픽로 424) was built for the 1988 Seoul Olympics and is the district's largest green space. The dataset lists scenes from The Empress's Dignity, Penthouse 3, Master's Sun and Second Husband, which has a picnic date here. The Olympic Gymnastics Arena inside the park is listed for Imitation and Uncontrollably Fond, and BTS and GFRIEND content marks the park's lone tree as a photo spot.

Take Seoul Subway Line 5 or Line 9 to Olympic Park Station, Exit 3, for the World Peace Gate side, or Line 8 to Mongchontoseong Station, Exit 1, for the museum side. The grounds are large and open from early morning, so plan on at least an hour.

SOMA Museum of Art (소마미술관)

The SOMA Museum (서울특별시 송파구 위례성대로 51) sits inside Olympic Park and is listed as the workplace of Sung Deok-mi in Her Private Life. It is closed on Mondays, so check the schedule before you plan the stop. The lawn and concrete halls make an easy exterior photo.

Songridan-gil, Jamsil Han River Park and Local Food

Songridan-gil (송리단길)

Songridan-gil is the cafe and restaurant street east of Seokchon Lake, centered on Baekjegobun-ro 45-gil. KCISA entries here include Doyoseu 6-ga (도요스6가, 서울특별시 송파구 백제고분로45길 40 트윈레이크팰리스 1층), a seafood-bowl restaurant that Girl's Day's Hyeri visited, and Got-it Songridan-gil (갓잇 송리단길점, 서울특별시 송파구 백제고분로45길 4-14), which Monsta X members visited. Oh Tsuseiro Mushi (오쓰세이로무시, 서울특별시 송파구 백제고분로45길 30), a steamed-dish spot, also appears for BTS. Walk five to ten minutes from Seokchon Station (Lines 8 and 9). Hours shift, so check Naver Map first.

Jamsil Han River Park (잠실한강공원)

Jamsil Han River Park (서울특별시 송파구 잠실2동 한가람로 65) is listed for WJSN's One-Pick Road. It is a riverside park with bike paths and a convenience-store picnic culture. Ride Line 2 to Jamsil Saenae Station and finish with a short walk or taxi. Cheonho Bridge (천호대교, 서울특별시 송파구 풍납동) on the district's northern edge is also listed for The Spy Who Loved Me and Strongest Deliveryman.

Where to eat and have coffee

Namgyeong Makguksu (남경막국수, 서울특별시 송파구 백제고분로7길 52-29) is a buckwheat-noodle favorite on several TV food shows. Seodusan Dimsum (서두산 딤섬, 서울특별시 송파구 올림픽로32길 52) and Bongpiyang Bangi (봉피양 방이점, 서울특별시 송파구 양재대로71길 1-4) appear for gourmet shows around Bangi-dong. For coffee, the cafes along Seokchon Lake Road (석촌호수로) and the bakeries inside the mall are the easiest picks.

Half-Day Songpa Itinerary

Start at Jamsil Station around 10:00 for Lotte World Mall and the Lotte World Tower exterior, then walk through the connected passage to Lotte World for photos at the carousel area or the lakeside. By midday, step out to Seokchon Lake and walk the east-lake path toward Songridan-gil for lunch, about 20 minutes on foot.

In the early afternoon, ride Line 8 two stops west from Seokchon Station to Mongchontoseong Station, Exit 1, for Olympic Park and the SOMA Museum, then wander the paths toward the World Peace Gate. If you still have energy at sunset, return to Jamsil and finish at Seokchon Lake while the tower lights up. If you would rather skip the park, replace it with a stop at Jamsil Han River Park.

The whole route is mostly flat and fits a half-day without a car. Bring a transit card such as T-money. For hotels near Jamsil and Lotte World Tower, or for Seoul day tours, use the comparison links below. They help keep K-Spot Korea free for fans.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get to Lotte World Tower and Lotte World by subway?

Take Seoul Subway Line 2 or Line 8 to Jamsil Station. Exit 1 and 2 connect underground to Lotte World Mall at 서울특별시 송파구 올림픽로 300, and signs lead to Lotte World at 서울특별시 송파구 올림픽로 240.

Which dramas filmed in Songpa-gu?

The KCISA public dataset lists Goblin, Stairway to Heaven, The Greatest Love, Alice, Our Beloved Summer, Love Alarm, Search: WWW, Encounter, Penthouse 3 and The Empress's Dignity among Songpa locations, mostly around Lotte World, Seokchon Lake and Olympic Park.

What is Songridan-gil?

Songridan-gil is a cafe and restaurant street east of Seokchon Lake around 백제고분로45길 in Songpa-gu. Several K-pop idols are listed there. Walk about 5–10 minutes from Seokchon Station.

How long do I need to see Songpa's filming locations?

A half-day is enough for Lotte World Mall, Seokchon Lake and Songridan-gil, and a full day if you add Lotte World rides and Olympic Park together with the SOMA Museum.

Are the photos on this page from the actual shows?
