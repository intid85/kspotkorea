Dumulmeori in Yangpyeong: K-Drama Filming Location Guide (She Was Pretty, My Love from the Star)

Dumulmeori (두물머리), the riverside point in Yangpyeong where the North and South Han rivers become one, has 20 records in Korea's public KCISA filming dataset. This guide shows which scenes of She Was Pretty, My Love from the Star, The Beauty Inside, Two Cops and more were filmed on its banks, and how to get there by Gyeongui-Jungang Line from Seoul.

Why Dumulmeori Is a Favorite K-Drama Riverside

Dumulmeori (두물머리) means "the head of two waters". It is the spit of land in Yangseo-myeon, Yangpyeong-gun, Gyeonggi-do, where the North Han River (북한강) and the South Han River (남한강) meet and become the Han River that flows on through Seoul. Once a busy ferry landing where boats from upriver unloaded timber and goods for the capital, it is now a free riverside park with a huge old zelkova tree, lotus beds, a red-sailed traditional boat, and a wide, calm stretch of water framed by low mountains.

That calm water, the soft morning mist and the sense of being far from the city, while sitting only about an hour from central Seoul by train, make it a natural set for walks, confessions and farewells. Korea's public KCISA filming-location dataset (한국문화정보원 미디어 촬영지 데이터, published via 공공데이터포털) lists 20 records at Dumulmeori itself: 17 drama entries covering 15 different dramas, 1 film and 2 K-pop artist entries. Four more records belong to a TV-famous lotus-leaf hot dog stand and a rice-cake restaurant on the same road.

This guide covers only Dumulmeori: which scenes the public data places here, what to see on the riverbank, the exact Korean address for a taxi, how to get there from Seoul on the Gyeongui-Jungang Line, where to eat, and when to go for the famous mist.

What Was Filmed at Dumulmeori (Public Data)

Everything below comes from the KCISA records for 두물머리 and 양평 두물머리 (경기도 양평군 양서면 양수리 / 두물머리길). Where a record is short or vague, we only repeat what it says and do not add scene details we cannot verify.

Confessions and romantic walks

The best-known record is She Was Pretty (그녀는 예뻤다), which has two entries. In episodes 13 and 14, after a picnic, Ji Sung-joon (Park Seo-joon) confesses his everlasting love to Kim Hye-jin (Hwang Jung-eum) here, and the dataset separately describes Dumulmeori as the location of the couple's kiss scene. In Goddess of Marriage (결혼의 여신), episode 4, Song Ji-hye (Nam Sang-mi) and Kang Tae-wook (Kim Ji-hoon) stroll along the river while getting to know each other, and in First Love (첫사랑), the 1990s classic, Sung Chan-hyuk (Choi Soo-jong) and Lee Seung-yeon (Lee Hyo-jung) come here on a date in episode 23.

In the final scene of episode 16 of Sweet Stranger and Me (우리집에 사는 남자), Hong Na-ri (Soo Ae) and Go Nan-gil (Kim Young-kwang) walk away hand in hand along the riverbank. Discovery of Love (연애의 발견) also lists Dumulmeori as a place Jung Yu-mi and Eric Mun's characters visit, and Let's Eat 3 (식샤를 합시다 3) used it as a scenic stop.

Starry nights, rituals and goodbyes

In episode 21 of My Love from the Star (별에서 온 그대), crowds gather in this riverside park to watch the meteor shower, with stars flying across the sky like UFOs. The Beauty Inside (뷰티 인사이드) has two entries: in episode 9, Han Se-gye (Seo Hyun-jin) undergoes a shaman's gut ritual here while her best friend Yoo Woo-mi (Moon Ji-in) and Ryu Eun-ho (Ahn Jae-hyun) watch, and another record notes Lee Min-ki and others talking on a bench.

Several dramas use the river for heavier moments. In Two Cops (투깝스), episode 20, Cha Dong-tak (Jo Jung-suk) comes here to say goodbye to his best friend Lee Doo-sik, killed in prison. In Graceful Family (우아한 가), episode 16, the family pays final respects to Mo Wan-soo (Lee Kyu-han), whose resting place is here in the story. In Undercover (언더커버), episode 16, Han Jung-hyun (Ji Jin-hee) and Choi Yeon-soo (Kim Hyun-joo) visit together.

More dramas, a film and K-pop

In Melting Me Softly (날 녹여주오), episode 8, producer Ma Dong-chan (Ji Chang-wook) scouts the park as a filming location. In When I Was the Most Beautiful (내가 가장 예뻤을 때), episode 1, art teacher Oh Ye-ji (Im Soo-hyang) brings her class here for an outdoor drawing lesson. Bride of the Century (백년의 신부) episode 2 has Choi Il-do (Choi Il-hwa) come here thinking of a woman in a wedding dress, and in Golden Bride (황금신부) episode 4, Kang Woo-nam (Kang Shin-il) brings his son and Jinju Nguyen (Lee Young-ah) here the day before their arranged wedding.

The 2016 comedy film Luck-Key (럭키) used Dumulmeori as a natural backdrop. On the K-pop side, the dataset lists NU'EST, after Minhyun visited Yangpyeong with his mother on I Live Alone (나 혼자 산다), and TWICE, who planted rice here on the KBS show 6시 내고향.

Walking Dumulmeori: The Point, the Frame and the Lotus Paths

Dumulmeori is compact. A relaxed loop of the riverbank takes 45 to 90 minutes, longer if you continue across the pontoon bridge to Semiwon. The paths are flat and mostly unpaved, so it is easy walking for all ages.

The zelkova tree and the boat at the point

At the tip of the spit stands a giant zelkova tree that local tourism sources describe as more than 400 years old, once a landmark for boatmen. Nearby, a wooden boat with a reddish-brown sail (황포돛배) is moored as a reminder of the old ferry days. These two, with the water behind them, are the image most dramas reach for.

The photo frame

A large wooden picture frame stands on the riverbank so you can "frame" the river and mountains. Expect a short queue on weekend afternoons; early morning is much quieter.

Lotus beds and the riverside path

Lotus beds line the shallow water near the point. They bloom in midsummer, but even in autumn the dried leaves and reflections are photogenic. A path follows the water past benches like the one in The Beauty Inside record, then continues to the Baedari pontoon bridge (배다리), a recreation of the boat bridge used in the Joseon era, which crosses to the Semiwon (세미원) water garden. Dumulmeori is free; Semiwon charges admission and keeps its own hours.

Address, Hours and How to Get There

Exact address for a taxi

The simplest option is to show the driver 두물머리. The KCISA records list the location as 경기도 양평군 양서면 양수리 and 경기도 양평군 양서면 두물머리길. Typing 두물머리 into Naver Map, KakaoMap or Kakao T brings up the right place and its parking lot. From Yangsu Station, a taxi takes about 5 minutes.

Opening hours and fees

According to the public data, Dumulmeori is open 24 hours a day, every day of the year (매일 00시 - 24시, 연중무휴). It is an open riverside area with no admission fee. The phone number in the record is 031-775-8700, the local tourist information line (Korean). Parking is available nearby, but lots fill up on weekends and fees may apply.

By train: Gyeongui-Jungang Line to Yangsu Station

Take the Gyeongui-Jungang Line (경의중앙선) toward Yongmun or Jipyeong and get off at Yangsu Station (양수역). From Cheongnyangni or Wangsimni it takes roughly 45 to 60 minutes, and the fare works with T-money. Some trains turn back before Yangsu, so check the destination on the platform board. From the station, walking to Dumulmeori takes about 25 to 30 minutes on flat roads and riverside paths. Use Naver Map or KakaoMap for walking directions, because Google Maps does not give full walking routes in Korea.

The Semiwon route

A popular alternative is to walk about 10 to 15 minutes from Yangsu Station to Semiwon (세미원, 양수로 93), go through the garden, and cross the Baedari pontoon bridge to Dumulmeori. Semiwon has an admission fee and, according to its official information, closes on Mondays from November to March; the bridge can also close in bad weather or high water. Check before you go.

By bike

Yangsu-ri sits on the Namhangang bike path. Riders often cross the old North Han River Railway Bridge (북한강철교), now a walking and cycling bridge next to Yangsu Station. The KCISA data notes that Park Shin-hye and Kim Rae-won's characters ride bikes across it in episode 2 of Doctors (닥터스).

Where to Eat and Drink Near Dumulmeori

The KCISA dataset also lists TV-featured snacks and restaurants in Yangseo-myeon. Hours below come from the public data and may have changed, so check Naver Map before you go.

The snack everyone buys is the lotus-leaf hot dog at Dumulmeori Yeon Hot Dog (두물머리연핫도그, 양서면 두물머리길 103-8), a stand on the road to the point. The data lists it on 2TV Saengsaeng Information, Omniscient Interfering View (전지적 참견시점, ep. 43) and MBC Power Magazine, open daily 10am to 6pm. Next door, Narutoga (나루터가, 두물머리길 101) appeared on VJ Special Forces for its rice cakes and is listed as open daily 10:30am to 8pm.

For a meal near Yangsu Station, the dataset includes Somunnan Yeon Kalguksu (소문난연칼국수, 양수로152번길 2-1), featured on Amazing Saturday for lotus-leaf noodle soup, and Dumulgil Hanjeongsik (두물길한정식, 양수로 111 2층), featured on the same show for marinated crab set meals. Both are listed as open daily 10am to 8:30pm. Yangsu Garden (양수가든, 북한강로 4), an eel restaurant, appeared on Welcome, First Time in Korea? (어서와 한국은 처음이지) and closes on Tuesdays.

For coffee, Susu Cafe (수수 카페, 북한강로89번길 16) is where NU'EST's Minhyun took his mother on I Live Alone, listed as open 10am to 10pm.

Visit Tips: Best Time, Photos and Etiquette

Come at dawn for the mist. Dumulmeori is famous for the morning fog (물안개) that rises off the water, especially on cool, still mornings in spring and autumn. Photographers arrive before sunrise. The earliest trains will not always get you there in time, so a nearby stay or a taxi helps.

Sunset works too. If you cannot make dawn, late afternoon light on the water and the zelkova is lovely, and the riverbank is calmer after the day-trip crowds leave.

Seasons. Lotus blooms peak in July and August (Semiwon holds a lotus festival in summer). Autumn leaves turn in late October to early November. Winter is cold and windy by the water, but snow on the riverbank is beautiful.

Avoid weekend afternoons if you can. The photo frame and the hot dog stand get long lines, and parking fills up. Weekday mornings are the quietest.

Respect the place. Stay on the paths around the lotus beds, do not climb the old tree or the boat, keep drones grounded unless you have checked local rules, and take your trash with you. Yangsu-ri is a living village, so keep noise down near homes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which K-dramas were filmed at Dumulmeori?

The KCISA public dataset lists 15 dramas at Dumulmeori, including She Was Pretty, My Love from the Star, The Beauty Inside, Melting Me Softly, Two Cops, Sweet Stranger and Me, When I Was the Most Beautiful, Undercover, Discovery of Love and Goddess of Marriage, plus the film Luck-Key and entries for NU'EST and TWICE.

What is the address of Dumulmeori for a taxi?

Show 두물머리 to the driver or type it into Naver Map, KakaoMap or Kakao T. The public data lists 경기도 양평군 양서면 양수리 (두물머리길).

Is Dumulmeori free, and is it open at night?

Yes. The public data lists Dumulmeori as open 24 hours, every day of the year, and it is a free riverside area. Semiwon, the garden across the pontoon bridge, charges admission and has its own hours.

How do I get to Dumulmeori from Seoul?

Take the Gyeongui-Jungang Line to Yangsu Station (about 45 to 60 minutes from Cheongnyangni or Wangsimni), then walk about 25 to 30 minutes or take a 5-minute taxi. You can also walk through Semiwon and cross the Baedari pontoon bridge.

Where was the She Was Pretty confession scene filmed?

According to the KCISA dataset, the episode 13 and 14 scene in which Ji Sung-joon (Park Seo-joon) confesses his love to Kim Hye-jin (Hwang Jung-eum) after a picnic was filmed at Dumulmeori in Yangpyeong, Gyeonggi-do.
