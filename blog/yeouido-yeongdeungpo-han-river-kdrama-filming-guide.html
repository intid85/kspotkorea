Why Yeongdeungpo-gu Is Seoul's Han River K-Drama District

When a K-Drama needs a character to stare at the river at night, cross a bridge after a breakup or walk into a glass tower for a high-stakes meeting, the location team very often ends up in Yeongdeungpo-gu. The district covers Yeouido, the flat island in the Han River that holds Korea's National Assembly, its main broadcasters and its financial district, plus older neighborhoods to the south such as Mullae-dong and Yeongdeungpo station.

In the public filming-location dataset published by the Korea Culture Information Service Agency (KCISA), Yeongdeungpo-gu has 582 records. Of those, 480 come from TV dramas, 77 from variety and food shows, 21 from K-Pop artists and four from films. By place type, 358 are parks, bridges and public spaces, 137 are restaurants and 61 are hotels. In other words, this is a district for walking along the water and looking up at skylines rather than a cafe-hopping district.

The single most filmed place in the whole district is Seogang Bridge, with 87 records. Two other Han River bridges, Wonhyo Bridge with 40 and Mapo Bridge with 16, are also near the top, and Yeouido Hangang Park adds another 36. Few districts in Korea can match that concentration of river scenes.

The Han River Bridges: Seogang, Wonhyo and Mapo

Seogang Bridge (서강대교)

Seogang Bridge links the west end of Yeouido to Mapo-gu, and its red arch is easy to spot from the riverbank. According to the KCISA records, Choi Ung and Guk Yeon-su from Our Beloved Summer cross it on a date in episode 13, and Lee Sung-yeon from What's Wrong with Secretary Kim calls his parents here in episode 3 right after arriving back in Korea. The list also includes Designated Survivor: 60 Days, VIP, Winter Sonata and My Roommate Is a Gumiho.

The pedestrian walkway is open around the clock and free. The easiest way to see the bridge is from the riverside path in Yeouido Hangang Park at sunset, then walk onto the deck if you want the classic crossing shot.

Mapo Bridge (마포대교) and Wonhyo Bridge (원효대교)

Mapo Bridge sits at the east side of the park and appears in Hotel del Luna, where IU's character checks a gold bar, and in Uncontrollably Fond, where Kim Woo-bin's character tells Lim Ju-eun to stay away from him in episode 17. The dataset also lists PSY's Gentleman music video for the fountain scene. Wonhyo Bridge, a little further east, has 40 records including Misaeng, She Was Pretty and While You Were Sleeping.

Yeouido Hangang Park and Yeouido Park

Yeouido Hangang Park (한강공원 여의도지구)

This riverside park at 서울특별시 영등포구 여의동로 290 is where Seoul comes to picnic, ride rental bikes and order fried chicken delivered to the grass. In My Love from the Star episode 4, Do Min-joon cycles past the area near the Yeouido 119 rescue station. In More Than Friends episode 15, three best friends spend the night at the park's multi-plaza, and in Chief Kim episode 18, Kim Sung-ryong meets Seo Yul just after his release from prison. Get off at Yeouinaru Station on Line 5 and you are at the water in two minutes.

Yeouido Park (여의도공원)

Yeouido Park is the long green strip in the middle of the island, between the office towers. It has 22 records. Be Melodramatic fans know it well: in episode 11, Son Beom-soo confesses to Im Jin-joo here, and in episode 12 she proposes they start dating and kisses him. The dataset also lists A Gentleman's Dignity and Record of Youth, plus K-Pop content from Lovelyz, OH MY GIRL and INFINITE. The park is open 24 hours, and Yeouido Station on Lines 5 and 9 is right at its edge.

Saetgang Bridge, the National Assembly and Parc1

Saetgang Bridge (샛강다리), at 서울특별시 영등포구 여의도동 39, is a curved footbridge over the small Saetgang stream that runs along the south side of Yeouido. It has a quiet, slightly melancholy feel that suits the dramas filmed here. The dataset says the three Park brothers in My Mister share a drink at a tent bar at the foot of the bridge, Chae Young-shin and Seo Jung-hoo go on a fake date here in Healer episode 11, and two characters walk along it at night in Shooting Stars episode 15.

At the west end of the island, the domed National Assembly building (국회의사당, 서울특별시 영등포구 의사당대로 1) is the explosion site in episode 1 of Designated Survivor: 60 Days and the workplace of Lee Jung-jae's character in Chief of Staff. Signal and My Liberation Notes also filmed here. You can photograph the building from the lawns outside the gate.

In the financial district, Parc1 (파크원, 서울특별시 영등포구 여의대로 108) appears in Extraordinary Attorney Woo episode 2 and in The Penthouse. Its basement connects to The Hyundai Seoul department store, where the dataset lists LSR X STILL BOOKS on B2 as a shop BLACKPINK's Jisoo shared on Instagram.

Seonyudo Park and the Mullae Art Village

Seonyudo Park (선유도공원)

Seonyudo Park, at 서울특별시 영등포구 선유로 343, is a former water treatment plant turned into a garden on a small island west of Yeouido. Its concrete tanks covered in vines make it one of the most photogenic parks in Seoul. In Boys Over Flowers episode 21, Geum Jan-di and Yoon Ji-hoo enter a couples photo contest here, and in Princess Hours episode 14, Crown Princess Shin Chae-kyung comes here alone and gives a balloon to a boy who recognizes her. VIXX also shot a self-made calendar here. The park is open from 6 AM to midnight; walk about ten minutes from Seonyudo Station on Line 9.

Mullae Creative Village (문래창작촌)

South of Yeouido, Mullae-dong is a neighborhood of small metal workshops that now share their alleys with artists' studios, wine bars and cafes. The dataset lists the area at 서울특별시 영등포구 도림로128가길 13-8 as the spot where Won Bin's character calls Kim Sae-ron out in the film The Man from Nowhere, and where Jay Park shot his GANG remix music video. Come in the late afternoon when the workshops close their shutters and the bars open. Mullae Station on Line 2 is the closest stop.

Where to Eat: Drama-Famous Yeongdeungpo Restaurants

Unsan (운산, 서울특별시 영등포구 여의공원로 101) is the hardest-working restaurant in the dataset for this district, with 26 drama records. Characters from Crash Landing on You, Itaewon Class, SKY Castle, My Mister, What's Wrong with Secretary Kim and Extraordinary Attorney Woo all have business lunches or family dinners here. It is open daily from 11:30 AM to 10 PM.

For a casual meal, Sambo Cheonggukjang (삼보 청국장, 서울특별시 영등포구 여의나루로 42 여의도종합상가 1층) is a fermented soybean stew restaurant the dataset says BTS's Jin visited, open daily 10 AM to 11 PM. In the evening, Yeouido Ua (여의도 우아, 서울특별시 영등포구 여의나루로 42) is the after-work pub from Because This Is My First Life episode 1, Law School episode 16 and Again My Life episode 5, open Monday to Saturday from 5 PM to midnight. Always confirm current hours on Naver Map before you go, since some places may have changed since the data was recorded.

Half-Day and One-Day Yeongdeungpo K-Spot Routes

Half-day Yeouido loop (about 4 hours)

Start at Yeouido Station and walk through Yeouido Park, then have lunch at Unsan or Sambo Cheonggukjang. Continue to Parc1 and The Hyundai Seoul, then walk north to Yeouido Hangang Park for the late afternoon. Finish with sunset views of Seogang Bridge and Mapo Bridge from the riverside path.

Full day with Seonyudo and Mullae

Begin at Seonyudo Park in the morning when the gardens are quiet, take Line 9 to National Assembly Station to see the dome, and spend the middle of the day on Yeouido. Stop at Saetgang Bridge on the way south, then end the day in Mullae Creative Village for dinner and drinks among the workshop alleys.

Show your taxi driver the Korean address for each stop; all of them are within a 15 to 20 minute ride of each other. Many of the riverside spots look best after dark, so leave the bridges for last.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the bridge from Our Beloved Summer and What's Wrong with Secretary Kim?

It is Seogang Bridge (서강대교), which links west Yeouido in Yeongdeungpo-gu to Mapo-gu. The walkway is open 24 hours and free; the best view is from Yeouido Hangang Park, a short walk from Yeouinaru Station on Line 5.

Where did Be Melodramatic film its confession scene?

The KCISA dataset lists Yeouido Park (여의도공원, 서울특별시 영등포구 여의공원로 68) for episodes 11 and 12. The park is open 24 hours and sits next to Yeouido Station on Lines 5 and 9.

Where is the bridge bar from My Mister?

The dataset places the tent-bar scene at the foot of Saetgang Bridge (샛강다리) on the south side of Yeouido, address 서울특별시 영등포구 여의도동 39. The nearest stop is Saetgang Station on Line 9.

Can I visit the Boys Over Flowers park in Seoul?

Yes. Seonyudo Park (선유도공원, 서울특별시 영등포구 선유로 343) is free and open from 6 AM to midnight. Walk about ten minutes from Seonyudo Station on Line 9.
