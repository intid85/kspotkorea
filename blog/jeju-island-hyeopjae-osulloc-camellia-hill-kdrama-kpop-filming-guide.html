Why Jeju Island Is a K-Drama and K-Pop Favorite

Jeju Island sits about an hour by air south of Seoul and is Korea's favorite weekend escape for beach days, volcanic landscapes and cafe hopping. It is also one of the densest K-content filming regions outside the capital: Korea's public KCISA filming-location dataset lists about 451 records with a Jeju address — 195 from K-Pop artists, 171 from variety shows, 82 from TV dramas and 3 from films.

This guide focuses on the places international fans actually plan a trip around: Hyeopjae and Hamdeok beaches, the O'Sulloc Tea Museum green-tea fields, Camellia Hill from BLACKPINK and BTS shoots, Seoyeon's House from Architecture 101 and SHINee, the Seogwipo healing forest from Crash Landing on You, and Seongeup Land from SEVENTEEN. Every stop includes the exact Korean address so you can paste it into Naver Map or KakaoMap, or show it to a taxi driver.

Jeju has no subway. Plan around Jeju International Airport (제주국제공항), Intercity buses, the Jeju City and Seogwipo tourist buses, and taxis. Renting a car or booking a day tour is the most realistic way to cover both the west coast and Seogwipo in one trip.

Hyeopjae and Hamdeok: Jeju's Most Filmed Beaches

Hyeopjae Beach (협재해수욕장)

Hyeopjae Beach (제주특별자치도 제주시 한림읍 한림로 329-10) on the west coast is Jeju's most filmed beach in the KCISA dataset, with seven records. Dae Jang Geum lists a seaside walk between Jang Geum and Min Jeong-ho here. The ending of Something in the Rain was shot on this sand. BLACKPINK's Jisoo and Lisa stopped here before leaving the island, SEVENTEEN filmed the Love Letter music video from the mini-album Al1 at Hyeopjae, and iKON jumped into the water during their School of Rock Jeju trip.

The water is famously turquoise on clear days, with Biyangdo Island on the horizon. From Jeju Airport, take a westbound bus toward Hallim or a taxi (about 40–50 minutes). Pair it with nearby Aewol cafes on the same coastal road.

Hamdeok Beach (함덕해수욕장)

Hamdeok Beach (제주특별자치도 제주시 조천읍 조함해안로 525) on the northeast coast has four KCISA records from Dreamcatcher, Min Kyung-hoon, SEVENTEEN and TXT. It is one of the easiest beaches to reach from Jeju Airport (about 30 minutes by taxi or bus toward Jocheon) and has a wide sandy shore, palm trees and cafes along the boardwalk. Go early in summer or on a weekday if you want space for photos.

Getting there: Use Naver Map or KakaoMap for real-time bus routes from the airport. Show taxi drivers 협재해수욕장 or 함덕해수욕장. Do not rely on Google Maps walking directions on Jeju — Korean apps are far more accurate for buses and rural roads.

O'Sulloc Tea Fields and Camellia Hill

O'Sulloc Tea Museum (오설록 티 뮤지엄)

The O'Sulloc Tea Museum (제주특별자치도 서귀포시 안덕면 신화역사로 15) sits in the middle of rolling green-tea fields in Andeok-myeon, west of Seogwipo. The KCISA dataset lists visits by BoA during a Running Man shoot, APRIL on their Jeju spring trip, and TWICE tasting green-tea ice cream on V LIVE TWICE TV3. Walk the outdoor paths between the tea bushes, then try the museum cafe's green-tea soft serve. Admission to the museum building is usually free; check hours on Naver before you go.

Camellia Hill (카멜리아힐)

Camellia Hill (제주특별자치도 서귀포시 안덕면 병악로 166) is a large botanical garden a short drive from O'Sulloc. The dataset ties it to BTS's Love Yourself poster shoot, BLACKPINK's Lisa visiting the camellia garden, and Girls' Generation YoonA's Innisfree commercial. Peak camellia season is winter into early spring, but the grounds stay photogenic year-round with palms, ponds and walking paths. Buy tickets at the gate or online; allow 1.5 to 2 hours.

Because both O'Sulloc and Camellia Hill sit in rural Andeok-myeon, combine them in one half-day with a taxi or rental car. From Jeju Airport the drive is about 40–50 minutes south.

Seoyeon's House, Crash Landing Forest and Saryeoni Trail

Seoyeon's House / Cafe Seochoyeon (서연의 집)

The cliffside house from the film Architecture 101 (건축학개론) is now Cafe Seochoyeon (제주특별자치도 서귀포시 남원읍 위미해안로 86). In the movie it is the house Seung-min builds for Seo-yeon; fans still come for the ocean view and the wooden exterior. SHINee's Taemin also filmed early We Got Married segments here, and the spot appears in Our Blues and Welcome to Samdal-ri related Jeju tourism. It is a working cafe — expect a wait on weekends — so order a drink and enjoy the Wimi coastline.

Seogwipo Healing Forest (치유의 숲)

Jeju's Seogwipo Healing Forest (제주특별자치도 서귀포시 산록남로 2271) is listed in KCISA as the Crash Landing on You forest where Yoon Se-ri parachutes in and meets Ri Jeong-hyeok. The real park is a managed forest trail on the southern slope of Hallasan. Trails are easy to moderate; wear comfortable shoes and check trail-open status on rainy or windy days. A taxi from central Seogwipo takes about 20–30 minutes.

Saryeoni Forest Path (사려니 숲길)

Nearby on the Jeju City side, Saryeoni Forest Path (제주특별자치도 제주시 조천읍 교래리 산 137-1) appears in Secret Garden when Hyun Bin's character searches the woods for Ha Ji-won, and as an IU Samdasoo commercial location and AKMU vlog stop. It is a flat, shaded boardwalk-style trail — one of the easiest Hallasan-area walks for visitors without hiking gear.

Seongeup Land, Folk Village and Aewol Cafe Street

Seongeup Land and Seongeup Folk Village (성읍랜드 · 성읍민속마을)

Seongeup Land (제주특별자치도 서귀포시 표선면 번영로 2650) is a theme park with horseback riding, ATVs and go-karts. The KCISA dataset lists six artist visits, including SEVENTEEN's Hot Zil shoot, EXO's Chanyeol, Sehun and D.O. on karts, Apink's Remember music video, Loco's Summer Go Loco MV, Shinhwa on Battle Trip and fromis_9. Next door, Seongeup Folk Village preserves thatched Jeju houses and stone walls — free to walk and perfect for photos after the park.

Aewol cafes: Monsant de Aewol and Bomnal (몽상드애월 · 봄날)

On the northwest coast, Monsant de Aewol (몽상드애월, 제주특별자치도 제주시 애월읍 애월북서길 56-1) has four KCISA records tied to G-Dragon, IU, Gray and APRIL — it was long known as a cafe associated with G-Dragon. A short drive away, Cafe Bomnal (봄날, 제주특별자치도 제주시 애월읍 애월로1길 25) is listed for TVXQ Yunho. Both sit on cliff-and-coast cafe streets that are easy to combine with Hyeopjae Beach.

Where to Eat: TV-Famous Jeju Restaurants

SEVENTEEN ate grilled hairtail at Choonshimi-ne main branch (춘심이네 본점, 제주특별자치도 서귀포시 안덕면 창천중앙로24번길 16) during Hot Zil — a good lunch stop near Camellia Hill. In Jungmun, Saekdal Restaurant (색달식당 중문갈치조림구이 본점, 제주특별자치도 서귀포시 예래로 255-18) is famous for whole grilled and braised hairtail and has appeared on food TV shows.

Near Jeju City, Yurine Restaurant (유리네식당, 제주특별자치도 제주시 연북로 146) is listed as a BTS Suga family favorite. On the Aewol coast, Noraba (노라바, 제주특별자치도 제주시 애월읍 구엄길 100) is known for seafood ramyeon. Hours change seasonally — always check Naver Map before you go, and show the Korean name to staff if English menus are limited.

One- to Two-Day Jeju K-Spot Itinerary

Fly into Jeju International Airport (제주특별자치도 제주시 공항로 2) in the morning. Pick up a rental car or pre-book a private driver, then drive west to Hyeopjae Beach for photos and a short swim. Continue along the Aewol coast for coffee at Monsant de Aewol or Cafe Bomnal, then head south to O'Sulloc Tea Museum and Camellia Hill for the afternoon.

Stay overnight in Seogwipo or Jungmun. On day two, visit Seoyeon's House cafe in Namwon-eup, then Seogwipo Healing Forest or Saryeoni Forest Path. If you have SEVENTEEN on your list, add Seongeup Land and walk Seongeup Folk Village afterward. End at Hamdeok Beach on the way back toward the airport.

Without a car, use airport limousines and tourist buses plus taxis between clusters. Show drivers the Korean addresses in this guide. For hotels in Jungmun, Seogwipo or near Hamdeok, compare stays with the hotel search widgets on this page — they help keep K-Spot Korea free for fans.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get from Jeju Airport to Hyeopjae Beach?

Hyeopjae Beach (제주특별자치도 제주시 한림읍 한림로 329-10) is about 40–50 minutes west of Jeju International Airport by taxi or westbound bus toward Hallim. Use Naver Map or KakaoMap for live bus times; Google Maps is unreliable for Jeju buses.

Where is the Architecture 101 house on Jeju?

It is Cafe Seochoyeon, also called Seoyeon's House (서연의 집, 제주특별자치도 서귀포시 남원읍 위미해안로 86). It is a working cafe on the Wimi coast in Seogwipo. SHINee also filmed here.

Is Camellia Hill the same place as O'Sulloc?

No. Camellia Hill (카멜리아힐, 제주특별자치도 서귀포시 안덕면 병악로 166) is a paid botanical garden. O'Sulloc Tea Museum (제주특별자치도 서귀포시 안덕면 신화역사로 15) is a short drive away in the same Andeok-myeon area. Visit both in one half-day.

Where was Crash Landing on You filmed on Jeju?

The KCISA dataset lists Seogwipo Healing Forest (치유의 숲, 제주특별자치도 서귀포시 산록남로 2271) as the forest where Se-ri crash-lands. Other CLOY scenes were filmed outside Korea; this is the main Jeju public-data match.

Do I need a car for a Jeju filming-location trip?

A rental car or private driver is strongly recommended. Spots are spread between Jeju City, Aewol, Andeok-myeon and Seogwipo with limited subway-style transit. Buses work for single beaches near the airport, but taxis between rural sites add up quickly.
