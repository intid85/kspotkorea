This guide links Gamcheon, Eulsukdo, Molundae and the Dadaepo sunset into one Line 1 day.

Why Saha-gu Belongs on Your Busan Screen Trip

Saha-gu is the far western end of Busan, where the city's hills drop to the mouth of the Nakdong River and the open sea. It packs three very different landscapes into one district: the painted hillside lanes of Gamcheon Culture Village, the wide tidal flats of Dadaepo Beach and the forested headland of Molundae, and the reed beds and bird sanctuary of Eulsukdo. All three are reachable from Busan Metro Line 1, which ends at Dadaepo Beach Station.

Korea's public KCISA filming-location dataset (한국문화정보원 미디어 촬영지 데이터) lists 21 records with a Saha-gu address. Gamcheon Culture Village appears five times: as the neighborhood where Jung Kyung-ho's detective lodges in Life on Mars, the home of Kim Rae-won's Ji-o in Luca: The Beginning, and the place where Esom's Lee Young-jae grew up with her brother in The Third Charm, plus K-pop visits by THE BOYZ and fromis_9. Dadaepo Beach is where Lee Min-ho's King Lee Gon gallops his horse Maximus in episode 1 of The King: Eternal Monarch, and the Eulsukdo estuary shows up in Life on Mars, Money Flower and the film Will You Be There? The rest of the list is local TV food.

Gamcheon Culture Village: Life on Mars, Luca and The Third Charm

Gamcheon Culture Village (감천문화마을) grew from a hillside settlement built by refugees during the Korean War. The small houses were laid out in terraces so that each one keeps a view of the valley, and since an arts project began here around 2009 the walls, stairways and rooftops have filled with murals, sculptures and small galleries.

In the dataset, Life on Mars uses the village as the background of the lodging where Jung Kyung-ho's detective stays after he wakes up in 1988. In Luca: The Beginning, Gamcheon is where Kim Rae-won's Ji-o lives, and episode 1 has him chased through its narrow alleys. The Third Charm sets the teenage and twenty-something years of Lee Young-jae (Esom) and her brother Soo-jae (Yang Dong-geun) here. On the K-pop side, THE BOYZ members Kevin, New and Juhaknyeon visited on a Busan trip, and fromis_9's Jiwon and Chaeyoung came looking for murals on their own Busan tour.

How to visit

Start at the information center near the main entrance, pick up a village map, and follow the painted fish and arrows along the main lane. Popular stops include the Little Prince statue looking over the valley. The dataset lists visiting hours of 9am to 6pm in summer and 9am to 5pm in winter. This is still a residential neighborhood, so keep your voice down, stay on the public lanes and never photograph into windows or open doors.

Getting there

Take Busan Metro Line 1 to Toseong Station (토성역), leave by exit 6, and ride a small village bus (Saha-gu 1-1, 2 or 2-2) uphill to the Gamcheon Culture Village stop; the ride takes about 10 minutes. A taxi from Toseong or Nampo takes 10 to 15 minutes. For the driver, show 감천문화마을 or 부산광역시 사하구 감내2로 203. The village is steep, so many visitors ride up and walk down.

Dadaepo Beach: The King: Eternal Monarch and the Sunset Fountain

Dadaepo Beach (다대포해수욕장) sits where the Nakdong River meets the sea, so the beach is wide, shallow and very flat. At low tide the wet sand turns into a mirror for the sky, which is why it is best known as a sunset spot. That mirror-like sand is also what made it a natural choice for episode 1 of The King: Eternal Monarch, where Lee Min-ho's King Lee Gon rides his white horse Maximus along the shore. The dataset also records BTS member Jimin filming a V Live broadcast here on a trip home to Busan, and SF9's Chani, Dawon and Inseong visiting on Battle Trip.

Right behind the beach is the Dadaepo Sunset Fountain of Dreams (다대포 꿈의 낙조분수), a large round music fountain with lights and water jets set to songs in the evening. It runs seasonally, usually in the warmer months, and shows can be cancelled for weather, so check the Saha-gu or Visit Busan schedule before planning around it. In late autumn the beach itself is the show: bring a jacket and walk out on the sand at golden hour.

Next to the beach, the Dadaepo Gouni Eco Trail (고우니생태길) is a flat wooden boardwalk over the coastal wetland, good when the wind is strong on the open sand.

Getting there

Ride Line 1 to its last stop, Dadaepo Beach Station (다대포해수욕장역). The beach and fountain are a few minutes' walk from the station exits. From Nampo Station the ride takes about 30 minutes. By taxi, show 다대포해수욕장 or 부산광역시 사하구 몰운대1길 14.

Molundae and Jangnim Port: Cliffs, Pines and a Fishing Harbor

At the southern end of Dadaepo Beach, Molundae (몰운대) is a pine-covered headland that pushes out into the sea. Its name is often explained as a cliff that disappears into clouds and fog, and it is designated a Busan Metropolitan City Monument (No. 27). A loop of forest trails leads to cliffs of layered sedimentary rock called the Dadaepo Formation, with views of small islands offshore.

Molundae does not appear in the KCISA filming dataset, so treat it as the scenic extension of the Dadaepo filming spot rather than a drama location. The walk from the beach to the end of the headland and back takes about one to one and a half hours at an easy pace. Wear shoes with grip, because the rocks near the water can be slippery, and stay behind the safety lines on the cliffs. For a taxi, show 몰운대 or 부산광역시 사하구 다대동 산144.

Jangnim Port: NCT's Battle Trip stop

Between Hadan and Dadaepo, Jangnim Port (장림포구, 장림로93번길 72) is a small fishing harbor whose brightly painted buildings along the water have earned it the nickname of Busan's little Venice. In the dataset it is where NCT's Jaemin and Jeno stopped during their Busan trip on Battle Trip. Jangnim Station on Line 1 is the closest stop, followed by a short taxi or a walk of about 20 minutes.

Eulsukdo and the Nakdong River Estuary: Life on Mars and Money Flower

Eulsukdo (을숙도) is a low island at the mouth of the Nakdong River, and the estuary around it is one of Korea's best-known stopovers for migratory birds. November through February is the main season for wintering ducks, swans and other water birds, so this trip timing works well. The Nakdong Estuary Eco Center (낙동강하구에코센터, 낙동남로 1240) has exhibits, observation windows and telescopes over the wetland; the dataset lists it as open Tuesday to Sunday from 9am to 6pm and closed on Mondays.

The reed beds here have a strong screen history. In the dataset, Life on Mars uses the estuary as the scene where a village chief is found murdered, with the tall reeds as backdrop. In episode 1 of Money Flower, Park Se-young's character Na Mo-hyeon works as a guide in the bird sanctuary on Eulsukdo. In the 2016 film Will You Be There?, the eco center is where Tae-ho goes to find Yeon-ah after reading Soo-hyun's letter.

Getting there

Take Line 1 to Hadan Station (하단역), then a city bus across the Nakdong River bridge toward Eulsukdo, or a taxi of about 10 minutes. For the driver, show 낙동강하구에코센터 or 부산광역시 사하구 낙동남로 1240.

Where to Eat in Saha-gu: TV-Famous Local Restaurants

Saha-gu's TV food picks are neighborhood places with Korean-only menus. Yeongjin Dwaeji Gukbap (영진돼지국밥 본점, 하신번영로157번길 39) is the pork-and-rice soup house that Day6's Sungjin recommended; the dataset lists it as open 9:30am to 9:30pm but also marks Sunday as a closing day, so check before going. Bokseong Banjeom (복성반점, 하신중앙로 289) is the spicy seafood jjamppong noodle shop from episode 9 of Baek Jong-won's Top 3 Chef King, open 11am to 9pm and closed on Tuesdays.

For seafood, Namhae Dalin Hoetjip (남해달인횟집, 사리로 40) serves raw-fish set meals featured on Morning Wide, Live Today Morning and VJ Special Forces, and opens Tuesday to Sunday from noon. Naekigo (내키고, 낙동대로563번안길 35-1) specializes in grilled and raw mackerel from Live Tonight and opens in the evening. Song Bujang Yeontan Galbi (송부장연탄갈비, 하신번영로167번길 6) from The Master of Living grills beef ribs from 5pm, Monday to Saturday. For dessert and coffee, small cafes with rooftop seating line the main lane of Gamcheon Culture Village.

A Half-Day Saha-gu Itinerary

Begin at Toseong Station around 10am and ride the village bus up to Gamcheon Culture Village. Spend about two hours on the lanes and viewpoints, then walk or ride back down. Take Line 1 west for a pork soup or jjamppong lunch near Hadan or Sinpyeong. In the early afternoon, visit the Nakdong Estuary Eco Center on Eulsukdo for the birds and reed beds (skip this on Mondays). Continue by Line 1 to Dadaepo Beach Station, walk the Molundae trail in the late afternoon, and finish on the sand at Dadaepo for sunset. Allow six to seven hours in total.

If you only have a few hours, choose Gamcheon in the morning and Dadaepo at sunset; both are on Line 1 and about 30 minutes apart. Check opening hours on Naver Map or KakaoMap before you go, because the dataset times may have changed, and remember that the fountain shows are seasonal.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which K-dramas were filmed at Gamcheon Culture Village?

Korea's public KCISA dataset lists Life on Mars, Luca: The Beginning and The Third Charm at Gamcheon Culture Village. K-pop groups THE BOYZ and fromis_9 also filmed visits there.

Where did Lee Min-ho ride the horse in The King: Eternal Monarch?

The dataset lists Dadaepo Beach in Saha-gu, Busan, as the beach where King Lee Gon rides his horse Maximus in episode 1. Take Busan Metro Line 1 to Dadaepo Beach Station.

How do I get to Gamcheon Culture Village by subway?

Take Line 1 to Toseong Station, leave by exit 6, and ride a village bus (Saha-gu 1-1, 2 or 2-2) up the hill for about 10 minutes. A taxi from Toseong takes about 10 minutes.

Is the Dadaepo sunset fountain running in winter?

The Dadaepo Sunset Fountain of Dreams runs seasonally, mainly in the warmer months, and shows can be cancelled for weather. Check the official Saha-gu or Visit Busan schedule before you go.

When is the best time to see birds at Eulsukdo?

Winter, roughly November to February, is the main season for migratory water birds at the Nakdong River estuary. The Nakdong Estuary Eco Center is closed on Mondays.
