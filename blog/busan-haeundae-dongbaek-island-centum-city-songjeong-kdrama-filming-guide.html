Busan Haeundae (Dongbaek Island, Centum City, Cheongsapo, Songjeong): K-Drama & K-Pop Filming Spots Guide

Haeundae-gu has 64 records in Korea's public KCISA filming-location dataset, including five locations from The King: Eternal Monarch. This guide links Haeundae Beach, Dongbaek Island, Centum City's Busan Cinema Center and BEXCO, Dalmaji-gil, Cheongsapo and Songjeong Beach into one subway-and-beach-train day, with Korean addresses for every stop.

Why Haeundae Is Busan's Biggest Screen Stage

Haeundae-gu is the district most travelers picture when they think of Busan: a 1.5 km curve of pale sand, a skyline of glass apartment towers, and a coastal road that winds east through fishing villages to a surf beach. Korea's public KCISA filming-location dataset lists 64 records with a Haeundae-gu address: 28 from variety and food shows, 24 from idol and artist visits, 10 from TV dramas and 2 from films. The single most-filmed drama is The King: Eternal Monarch, with five separate Haeundae locations.

What makes the district easy for fans is how compact it is. Busan Metro Line 2 runs along the coast through Centum City, Dongbaek and Haeundae stations, and the Donghae Line covers BEXCO and Songjeong. In one day you can walk the island where the drama placed its imperial palace, stand where Lee Min-ho fought outside the film festival theater, and finish on the beach from a K-pop music video.

Haeundae Beach and Gunam-ro: Seagulls, Street Food and a New Year Stroll

The beach itself (부산광역시 해운대구 우동) appears in the dataset for 1 Night 2 Days, for fromis_9 member Saerom's Busan travel vlog, and for We Got Married, where CNBLUE's Jung Yong-hwa fed snacks to seagulls. Get off at Haeundae Station on Line 2 and walk about 5 minutes down Gunam-ro (구남로), the wide pedestrian boulevard that leads straight to the sand.

Gunam-ro Culture Plaza

The plaza along Gunam-ro is where, in episodes 9 and 10 of The King: Eternal Monarch, Lee Gon walks through Haeundae during the Lunar New Year. It is a pleasant, open space today, lined with busking spots and cafes. The side street Gunam-ro 41beon-gil is packed with spots from food shows like 6 O'Clock My Hometown, and Super Junior's Kyuhyun visited the tteokbokki shop Sanggugine (상국이네, 구남로41번길 40-1). Haeundae Traditional Market sits a block away for fish cakes and fried snacks.

Dongbaek Island and Marine City: The King's Imperial Palace

Walk west along the beach and you reach Dongbaek Island (동백섬, 부산광역시 해운대구 동백로 99), a small wooded headland now joined to the mainland. In The King: Eternal Monarch it is listed as the location of the imperial palace of the drama's Kingdom of Corea, and it also appears in episode 5 of Shopping King Louie, when Go Bok-sil travels to Busan, and in the dating show Heart Signal. A loop trail of about 1 km circles the island past pine trees, rocky coves, a lighthouse viewpoint and Nurimaru APEC House, the round summit hall built for the 2005 APEC meeting.

The trail is open all day and free. The closest subway stop is Dongbaek Station on Line 2; from Exit 1 it is about a 10 minute walk. Across the water is Marine City, the cluster of skyscrapers seen in many Busan night shots. The dataset lists Park Hyatt Busan (부산광역시 해운대구 마린시티1로 51) as the apartment with a private pool used by Chun Soo-ho in the drama Time.

Centum City: Busan Cinema Center, BEXCO and APEC Naru Park

Two Line 2 stops west of Dongbaek, Centum City is Busan's convention and culture district, and it holds four of The King's five Haeundae locations. Outside the Busan Cinema Center (영화의전당, 부산광역시 해운대구 수영강변대로 120), Lee Gon rides in with mounted guards and a SWAT team to rescue Jeong Tae-eul from Lee Lim's men. The building is the home of the Busan International Film Festival, and its giant cantilevered roof is lit with LED patterns after dark. It is open daily from 9am to 10pm.

BEXCO and the Busan Museum of Art

The BEXCO convention complex (부산광역시 해운대구 APEC로 55) plays Busan CTX Station in episode 5 of The King, where Tae-eul heads for a high-speed train to Seoul, and it plays the PEXCO convention center hosting the Quantum Time conference in Sisyphus: The Myth. Right next door, the Busan Museum of Art (부산광역시 해운대구 APEC로 58) is a spot RM of BTS shared on Twitter; it opens Tuesday to Sunday, 10am to 6pm.

APEC Naru Park and the Im Kwon-taek Film Museum

APEC Naru Park (부산광역시 해운대구 수영강변대로 93), a riverside lawn along the Suyeong River, is where Tae-eul spots the exhibition poster for Lee Gon. Nearby, the Im Kwon-taek Film Museum (센텀중앙로 55) is where Ha Moon-soo and Lee Kang-doo meet for their last date in Just Between Lovers; the dataset lists it as open Tuesday to Saturday, noon to 6pm.

Dalmaji-gil and Cheongsapo: BTS Views and Clam Grills

East of the beach, the road climbs onto Dalmaji Hill. Dalmaji-gil (달맞이길, 부산광역시 해운대구 달맞이길 190) is the road where BTS's V and actor Park Seo-joon stopped for photos, and the road Apink used in the music video for Hopefully Sky (하늘바라기), the solo debut of Apink's Jung Eun-ji. The hill is lined with cafes and galleries looking down on the sea. Near the bottom of the hill, Saeachim Sikdang (새아침식당, 달맞이길62번길 28) is the grilled-clam restaurant where Running Man episode 328 filmed and TWICE ate with the cast.

Over the hill, Cheongsapo (청사포) is a small fishing harbor with a red and white pair of lighthouses. The cafe Doré Doré Cheongsapo (도레도레 청사포점, 청사포로 137) was featured on Battle Trip, and Hajinine (하진이네, 청사포로 151), a late-night grilled-shellfish spot, appeared when Block B's Jaehyo visited. The easiest way here is the Haeundae Blue Line Park beach train, which runs along the old coastal rail line from Mipo near Haeundae Beach to Cheongsapo and Songjeong.

Songjeong Beach: Will You Be There and Apink's Music Video

Songjeong Beach (송정해수욕장, 부산광역시 해운대구 송정동 712-2) is quieter and shallower than Haeundae and is known as Busan's beginner surf beach, with board rental shops along the promenade. In the 2016 film Will You Be There?, it is the seaside where the younger Soo-hyun and Tae-ho talk 30 years in the past. The Hopefully Sky music video also filmed here and at the old, closed Songjeong Station building (송정중앙로6번길 60), a small heritage station a short walk inland.

Songjeong Station on the Donghae Line is about 10 minutes on foot from the sand, or ride the Blue Line beach train from Cheongsapo.

Half-Day Haeundae K-Drama Itinerary

Start at Centum City Station around 10am and walk past the Busan Cinema Center, the Busan Museum of Art and BEXCO, then cross to APEC Naru Park. Ride Line 2 two stops to Dongbaek, walk the island loop, and follow the beach east to Gunam-ro for lunch at Haeundae Market. In the afternoon, board the Blue Line Park beach train at Mipo, get off at Cheongsapo for coffee by the lighthouse, and continue to Songjeong for sunset on the beach. Allow five to six hours with stops.

For food, the dataset points to Busan staples: dwaeji gukbap (pork soup with rice) at Uiryeong Sikdang (의령식당, 우동1로50번길 15), visited by Dynamic Duo's Gaeko; cod soup at Haeundae Giwajip Daegutang (달맞이길104번길 46), where G-Dragon ate; and grilled clams on Dalmaji Hill. Restaurants change, so confirm hours on Naver Map or KakaoMap before you go.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where was The King: Eternal Monarch filmed in Busan?

Korea's public KCISA dataset lists five Haeundae locations: Dongbaek Island (palace setting), Busan Cinema Center (rescue fight scene), BEXCO (Busan CTX Station), APEC Naru Park and the Gunam-ro culture plaza near Haeundae Beach.

How do I get to Haeundae from Busan Station?

Take Busan Metro Line 1 to Seomyeon, transfer to Line 2 and ride to Haeundae Station. The trip takes about 50 minutes. A taxi takes around 30 to 40 minutes depending on traffic.

Is Dongbaek Island free to visit?

Yes. The coastal trail around Dongbaek Island is open all day and free. Nurimaru APEC House on the island has its own opening hours, so check before you go.

How do I get from Haeundae Beach to Cheongsapo and Songjeong?

The Haeundae Blue Line Park beach train runs from Mipo, at the east end of Haeundae Beach, through Cheongsapo to Songjeong. You can also take a short taxi over Dalmaji Hill or the Donghae Line to Songjeong Station.

Which BTS spots are in Haeundae?

The dataset lists Dalmaji-gil, where V and Park Seo-joon took photos; the Busan Museum of Art, shared by RM; and Haeundae Busan Hoetjip on Dalmaji-gil 62beon-gil, which j-hope remembered from his younger days.
