Why Gangnam-gu Is Seoul's K-Pop Dining District

If Jongno is where Korean dramas go for palaces and Mapo-gu is where they go for youth, Gangnam-gu is where they go for glamour. In the public filming-location dataset published by the Korea Culture Information Service Agency (KCISA), Gangnam-gu has 995 records, the third-highest count of any district in Korea after Jongno-gu and Mapo-gu.

The mix is very different from the old city. Of those 995 records, 584 are restaurants and 93 are cafes, while 229 are streets, shops and public spaces. Gangnam is where idols go out to eat, where agencies and broadcasters film variety segments, and where drama writers send their rich heirs and CEOs for dinner.

Works that appear most often in Gangnam-gu

Among music entries, BLACKPINK (29 records), Super Junior (26) and BTS (25) lead the dataset here, followed by TVXQ, SEVENTEEN and TWICE. On the drama side the records include Crash Landing on You, Goblin, What's Wrong with Secretary Kim, My Love from the Star, My Mister, Itaewon Class and Twenty-Five Twenty-One. Variety shows such as Omniscient Interfering View also filmed dozens of restaurant segments in the district.

Cheongdam: The Crash Landing on You and Goblin Block

The most rewarding single street for drama fans is Seolleung-ro 152-gil in Cheongdam-dong. At number 21 is Wolfgang's Steakhouse, which the KCISA record says appears in episode 1 of Crash Landing on You as the restaurant where Yoon Se-ri (Son Ye-jin) has dinner with her boyfriend Cha Sang-woo, and where the paparazzi photo of the couple is taken. The same restaurant is also listed for BLACKPINK, with Jisoo and Lisa posting visits on Instagram, and for NCT's Taeyong.

A few doors down at number 30 is Space Cheongdam, which the dataset lists as the lounge bar from episode 12 of Goblin where Yoo Deok-hwa (Yook Sung-jae) meets Kim Shin (Gong Yoo) and the Grim Reaper (Lee Dong-wook). The same address also appears as a filming spot for an episode of Run BTS.

To finish the Crash Landing on You trail, head to Cheongdam Station on Line 7. In episode 12, Geum Eun-dong waits for Ri Jeong-hyeok (Hyun Bin) at Exit 3, according to the dataset.

Apgujeong Rodeo & K-Star Road: BLACKPINK's Favorite Streets

Apgujeong-ro, the long avenue that runs through Apgujeong and Cheongdam, is packed with BLACKPINK entries. The dataset lists Louis Vuitton Maison Seoul (압구정로 454) as a cafe Rosé visited, the House of Dior Seoul (압구정로 464) as a place Lisa and Jisoo visited together in Lisa's YouTube video, and 10 Corso Como Cheongdam (압구정로 416) as a cafe Jennie posted on Instagram.

Right by Apgujeong Rodeo Station is K-Star Road (압구정로 339-3), a street lined with large art-toy figures of Hallyu stars. It is free, outdoors and one of the easiest photo stops in the district.

Secretary Kim and Itaewon Class

Fans of What's Wrong with Secretary Kim can look for the Sulwhasoo Dosan flagship (도산대로45길 18), which Kim Mi-so and Lee Young-joon walk past on their way to lunch, and RAUM (언주로 564), listed as the party venue from episode 1. Itaewon Class fans can find Queens Park Cheongdam (압구정로60길 22), which the dataset says plays Oh Soo-ah's restaurant in the drama.

Sinsa & Nonhyeon: Where BTS Trained and Ate

For ARMY, the area between Sinsa and Nonhyeon is the heart of the district. The dataset lists Hakdong Park (강남대로140길 47) as a spot from BTS's trainee days that also appeared in Rookie King, and Yoojung Sikdang (도산대로28길 14) as the members' regular restaurant when they were trainees.

Other records include Eunhaenggol Sinsa (강남대로152길 42), a sushi restaurant visited by Jimin and V that is also listed for BLACKPINK's Jisoo, and Uga (도산대로49길 22), where BTS held a dinner live stream after a Billboard award promise, which Jisoo is also recorded as visiting.

SEVENTEEN fans have their own stops nearby, including Aloha Poke Hakdong (논현로131길 24), posted on the group's official Instagram.

COEX and Gangnam Station

On the eastern side of the district, COEX (영동대로 513) is listed for Business Proposal, where Shin Ha-ri meets Kang Tae-moo at the hotel's sky lounge restaurant. Next door, Starfield COEX Mall (봉은사로 524) appears in the dataset as the filming spot for the performance-team version of SEVENTEEN's Adore U dance video. COEX is a good rainy-day stop, and it is reached from Samseong Station on Line 2 or Bongeunsa Station on Line 9.

Finally, Gangnam Station itself (강남대로 396) is in the dataset for My Love from the Star. In episode 5, Do Min-joon (Kim Soo-hyun) rides past a station exit on a bicycle as Cheon Song-yi's billboard is taken down.

A One-Day Gangnam K-Spot Route and Practical Tips

A comfortable order is Sinsa and Hakdong in the morning, Apgujeong Rodeo and K-Star Road around lunch, Cheongdam's Seolleung-ro 152-gil in the afternoon, and COEX for the evening. The Suin-Bundang Line, Line 7 and Line 2 connect these areas, and taxis are easy to find.

Many of these places are working restaurants and cafes, and some are fine-dining spots that need reservations, so check opening hours and booking rules before you go. Addresses here come from the KCISA public dataset and businesses can move or close, so it is worth confirming on Naver Map or KakaoMap on the day. Please be respectful at private shops and do not block entrances for photos.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where was the Crash Landing on You steakhouse scene filmed?

According to the KCISA public filming dataset, the restaurant exterior in episode 1 is Wolfgang's Steakhouse at 서울특별시 강남구 선릉로152길 21 in Cheongdam-dong, Seoul.

Which BLACKPINK spots are in Gangnam?

Gangnam-gu has 29 BLACKPINK records in the dataset, including Louis Vuitton Maison Seoul, the House of Dior Seoul, 10 Corso Como Cheongdam and Wolfgang's Steakhouse, mostly along Apgujeong-ro and Dosan-daero.

Where can BTS fans go in Gangnam?

Popular entries include Hakdong Park from the members' trainee days, Yoojung Sikdang, their regular trainee-era restaurant, and Uga, where BTS held a dinner live stream after a Billboard award promise.

How do I get to COEX from Gangnam Station?

Take Line 2 from Gangnam Station to Samseong Station, which connects directly to COEX. Bongeunsa Station on Line 9 is another option.
