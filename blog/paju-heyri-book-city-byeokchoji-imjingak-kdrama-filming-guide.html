Paju, the city between Seoul and the North Korean border, has 533 records in Korea's public KCISA filming-location dataset, and 417 of them come from TV dramas. Its book-publishing town, Heyri art village, quiet gardens and border parks give K-Drama crews modern galleries, cafes and forests within an hour of Seoul. This guide links Paju Book City, Heyri, Byeokchoji Arboretum and Imjingak into one easy day trip with Korean addresses for taxis.

Why Paju Is the K-Drama Day Trip North of Seoul

When a K-Drama needs a sleek publishing house, a gallery cafe with concrete walls, a European-style garden or a quiet temple at dusk, the location team very often drives north out of Seoul to Paju. The city sits on the Han and Imjin rivers right below the inter-Korean border, about an hour from central Seoul, and it packs a surprising mix of scenery into one place: Paju Book City, a planned town of architect-designed publishing offices; Heyri Art Village, a hillside of galleries, studios and cafes; and green escapes such as Byeokchoji Arboretum and the Imjingak peace park.

In the public filming-location dataset published by the Korea Culture Information Service Agency (KCISA), Paju-si has 533 records. Of those, 417 come from TV dramas, 79 from variety and food shows, 33 from K-Pop artists and four from films. By place type, 305 are parks, galleries and other public spaces, 119 are restaurants and 92 are cafes. That makes Paju a district for strolling between buildings and gardens, then stopping for coffee in a place you have already seen on screen.

The single most filmed place in the city is White Block, a gallery and cafe in Heyri, with 44 records. A rental filming studio in Tanhyeon-myeon has 40, the Gyeonggi English Village has 33, Farmers Table restaurant in Heyri has 32, the Forest of Wisdom library has 31 and Byeokchoji Arboretum has 29.

Paju Book City: Libraries, Publishers and Gallery Cafes

Forest of Wisdom (지혜의 숲)

The Forest of Wisdom, at 경기도 파주시 회동길 145, is a library with bookshelves that rise to the ceiling, and it is open daily from 10 AM to 8 PM. It is one of the most recognizable sets in modern K-Drama. According to the KCISA records, in What's Wrong with Secretary Kim episode 4 Lee Young-joon and Kim Mi-so wander the library during a blackout, and he lights the shelves with his phone so she can read her favorite book. She Was Pretty, Hotel del Luna, The King: Eternal Monarch, The World of the Married, Chicago Typewriter and My Unfamiliar Family also filmed here.

Publishers and cafes around Hoedong-gil

Walk south along Hoedong-gil and you will recognize more buildings. Tteuindol Publishing (뜨인돌출판사, 경기도 파주시 회동길 337-9), with its cross-shaped glass staircase, appears in Search: WWW episode 5, It's Okay, That's Love and It's Okay to Not Be Okay. Mediterium (메디테리움, 경기도 파주시 회동길 338) becomes the Search Korea office in Stranger 2 and the Julie Books building in Yumi's Cells 2. Art Gallery Cafe (아트갤러리카페, 경기도 파주시 회동길 445-2) is where Kim Mi-so meets Young-joon's mother in Secretary Kim episode 3, and Cafe Paper and Tree (카페 종이와 나무, 경기도 파주시 회동길 445-1) shows up in Romance Is a Bonus Book episode 11 and Business Proposal.

Heyri Art Village: Galleries, Cafes and Drama Restaurants

White Block (화이트블럭)

White Block, at 경기도 파주시 탄현면 헤이리마을길 72, is a white gallery building with a large cafe facing a pond. The dataset lists the cafe as open 10:30 AM to 7 PM on weekdays and until 9 PM on weekends. In 18 Again episode 5, Choi Il-kwon meets Jung Da-jung here while Hong Dae-young watches from a distance, and in More Than Friends episode 8, Lee-soo visits his mother's exhibition in the gallery. On the Way to the Airport, Military Prosecutor Doberman, Legend of the Blue Sea, Misty and 30 But 17 also filmed here.

Adamas 253 (아다마스253)

This cafe and restaurant building at 경기도 파주시 탄현면 헤이리마을길 47 is where Park Do-kyung and Oh Hae-young first meet in Another Miss Oh episode 1, and where Kim Mi-so and Lee Young-joon come for comfort food in Secretary Kim episode 12. Man to Man and My Secret Hotel also used it. It is open daily from 11 AM to 9 PM.

Heyri itself is free to walk, and many of the small museums and galleries charge their own entry. Note that a number of Heyri businesses close on Mondays.

Byeokchoji Arboretum and the English Village

Byeokchoji Arboretum (벽초지수목원)

Byeokchoji, at 경기도 파주시 광탄면 부흥로 242, is a landscaped arboretum with a lotus pond, a willow-lined lake and formal European gardens. The dataset lists it as open daily from 9 AM to 5:30 PM. This is the garden where Kang Dan-i and Cha Eun-ho walk and kiss in the final scene of Romance Is a Bonus Book, and where Hong Ji-hong proposes to Yoo Hye-jung in Doctors episode 20. In The Mask, Choi Min-woo brings Byun Ji-sook here to listen to the wind and birds. In Park Chan-wook's film The Handmaiden, the count and Sook-hee argue over their secret deal in these gardens.

K-Pop fans will recognize it too: the KCISA dataset lists Red Velvet's Psycho music video, VIXX, Apink, B1A4 and BTOB music videos, a (G)I-DLE teaser, and a visit Jennie of BLACKPINK posted on Instagram. There is no train station nearby, so take a taxi from Geumchon or Munsan Station on the Gyeongui-Jungang Line.

Gyeonggi English Village Paju Campus (경기영어마을 파주캠퍼스)

This mock European town at 경기도 파주시 탄현면 얼음실로 40 is a favorite stand-in for foreign cities. In Search: WWW, Cha Hyun and Seol Ji-hwan watch the sunrise from its parking lot, and in More Than Friends episode 14 it doubles as an American city where Lee-soo calls Woo-yeon at night. The dataset also lists TWICE's Cheer Up music video, EXO's Miracles in December and Super Junior's Magic. It is about ten minutes by taxi from Heyri.

Yakcheonsa Temple, Unjeong Lake Park and Imjingak

Yakcheonsa (약천사), at 경기도 파주시 교하로681번길 118, is a small hillside temple near the Unjeong new town. In My Liberation Notes episode 8, Mr. Gu and Yeom Mi-jeong come here in the evening and watch a rainbow appear in front of them. Her Private Life, Melting Me Softly and My ID Is Gangnam Beauty also filmed here. Nearby, Unjeong Lake Park (운정호수공원, 경기도 파주시 경의로 1151) is open 24 hours and appears in The Devil Judge and How to Hate You.

Further north, Imjingak (임진각, 경기도 파주시 문산읍 임진각로 164) and the Peace Nuri park beside it sit just south of the Civilian Control Line. The dataset says Woo Seo-ri and Gong Woo-jin share their first kiss at Imjingak Peace Nuri in 30 But 17, and the film 1987 opens with Kim Yoon-seok's character performing an ancestral rite here. Take a taxi from Munsan Station, the end of the Gyeongui-Jungang Line commuter service.

Where to Eat: Drama-Famous Paju Restaurants

Farmers Table (파머스테이블, 경기도 파주시 탄현면 헤이리마을길 59-77) in Heyri has 32 drama records. The Hong family has dinner here in 18 Again episode 18, and Kim Mi-so and Lee Sung-yeon eat here in Secretary Kim episode 5. The dataset lists it as closed on Mondays. Also in Heyri, Mokushura (모쿠슈라, 경기도 파주시 탄현면 헤이리마을길 54) is where Yumi takes Woo-gi for lunch in Yumi's Cells episode 2, and it appears in Our Beloved Summer and Business Proposal. It opens daily from 10:30 AM to 9:30 PM.

For a cheaper bowl, Yonggung Kalguksu (용궁칼국수, 경기도 파주시 탄현면 새오리로 59-52) serves seafood knife-cut noodles. Vincenzo and Hong Cha-young have lunch here with a prosecutor in Vincenzo episode 14, and the dataset notes a signed autograph from Sunny of Girls' Generation from a Running Man shoot. Always confirm current hours on Naver Map before you go, since some places may have changed since the data was recorded.

Half-Day and One-Day Paju K-Spot Routes

Half-day Book City and Heyri (about 5 hours)

Take bus 2200 from Hapjeong Station in Seoul to Paju Book City and start at the Forest of Wisdom when it opens. Walk Hoedong-gil past the publishers and stop for coffee at Art Gallery Cafe, then ride the same bus line a few stops north to Heyri. Have lunch at Farmers Table or Mokushura, then finish at White Block and Adamas 253.

Full day with Byeokchoji and Imjingak

A full Paju day works best by taxi or rental car, because the spots are spread out. Begin at Byeokchoji Arboretum in the morning when the light is soft, continue north to Imjingak for the border views, then come back south to Heyri for a late lunch and the gallery cafes. If you still have time, end at Yakcheonsa or Unjeong Lake Park at sunset.

Show your taxi driver the Korean address for each stop. Paju is large, so allow 20 to 40 minutes between the gardens, Heyri and Imjingak.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the library from What's Wrong with Secretary Kim?

It is the Forest of Wisdom (지혜의 숲) in Paju Book City, address 경기도 파주시 회동길 145. The KCISA dataset lists it as open daily from 10 AM to 8 PM. Bus 2200 from Hapjeong Station in Seoul stops nearby.

Where was the final scene of Romance Is a Bonus Book filmed?

The KCISA dataset places it at Byeokchoji Arboretum (벽초지수목원, 경기도 파주시 광탄면 부흥로 242), listed as open daily from 9 AM to 5:30 PM. Take a taxi from Geumchon or Munsan Station.

Where did Another Miss Oh's leads first meet?

The dataset lists Adamas 253 (아다마스253) in Heyri Art Village, address 경기도 파주시 탄현면 헤이리마을길 47, for episode 1. It is open daily from 11 AM to 9 PM.

Can I visit Paju as a day trip from Seoul?

Yes. Paju Book City and Heyri Art Village are about an hour from Hapjeong Station on bus 2200. Byeokchoji Arboretum and Imjingak are easier by taxi from Gyeongui-Jungang Line stations such as Geumchon and Munsan.
