Seongnam Bundang (Pangyo Techno Valley, Jeongja, Seohyeon, Yuldong Park): K-Drama Filming Spots Guide

Seongnam, just south of Seoul, has 202 records in Korea's public KCISA filming-location dataset, 135 of them from TV dramas, and 167 sit in Bundang-gu alone. This guide links Pangyo Techno Valley, the Baekhyeon-dong cafe street, Jeongja-dong, Tancheon Stream, Seohyeon and Yuldong Park into one easy subway-and-bus half-day, with Korean addresses for every stop.

Why Bundang Is Korea's Favorite Office-Romance Backdrop

Seongnam is the city directly south of Seoul's Gangnam, and its Bundang-gu district is a planned new town of tree-lined boulevards, glass office towers and quiet apartment blocks. Korea's public KCISA filming-location dataset lists 202 records with a Seongnam address: 135 from dramas, 51 from variety and food shows, 14 from idol and artist visits and 2 from films. Of those, 167 are in Bundang-gu, and the single most-filmed drama here is Still 17 (Thirty But Seventeen), with 13 entries.

The reason is easy to see once you arrive. Pangyo Techno Valley is home to Korea's game and internet companies, so whenever a drama needs a startup office, a sleek cafe or a bus stop for a late-night confession, scouts come here. You will recognize the look from Start-Up, Her Private Life, Because This Is My First Life and Run On.

Every stop below lists the exact Korean address, so you can paste it into Naver Map or KakaoMap or show it to a taxi driver. The dataset records where scenes were filmed. Many spots are working offices or private homes, so enjoy them from the street and keep noise down.

Pangyo Techno Valley: Start-Up, Her Private Life and Bus-Stop Kisses

U-Space Plaza (유스페이스)

In episode 2 of Start-Up, Seo Dal-mi (Bae Suzy) arranges to meet Nam Do-san (Nam Joo-hyuk) in this open plaza between office towers at 경기도 성남시 분당구 판교역로 233-1. The plaza also appears in Starry Night of Goho. Next door, Pangyo H Square (판교역로 231) shows up in A-Teen and Webtoon: Today's Webtoon.

Neowiz Building (네오위즈)

This is the most-filmed single address in Seongnam, with eight drama entries in the dataset. Its rooftop and exterior played Seong Deok-mi's (Park Min-young) rooftop apartment in Her Private Life, and it also appears in Military Prosecutor Doberman, Kkondae Intern and The Jugglers. The address is 경기도 성남시 분당구 대왕판교로645번길 14. A few streets away, the NCSOFT R&D Center cafeteria (대왕판교로644번길 12) stood in for a university canteen, and the building also appears in Still 17, Strong Woman Do Bong-soon and Psychometry He's Listening.

Pangyo Seven Venture Valley Bus Stop (판교세븐벤처벨리 정류장)

In the first episode of Because This Is My First Life, Yoon Ji-ho (Jung So-min) kisses Nam Se-hee (Lee Min-ki) on the bus just before getting off at this stop in 삼평동 626. The Hwarang (화랑육교) and Dong-an (동안육교) footbridges nearby appear in Suits, Kiss Sixth Sense, Business Proposal, Still 17 and Today's Webtoon, and are good spots for a skyline photo.

Getting there: take the Shinbundang Line (from Gangnam Station, about 15 minutes) or the Gyeonggang Line to Pangyo Station. The Hyundai Department Store Pangyo connects to the station, which is handy on a rainy day.

Baekhyeon-dong Cafe Street: Still 17, Vincenzo and Yumi's Cells

Just south of Pangyo Station, the low-rise Baekhyeon-dong cafe street (경기도 성남시 분당구 백현동 567-1) is lined with brunch cafes and boutiques. In episode 8 of Still 17, Gong Woo-jin (Yang Se-jong) has a run-in at one of the cafes here. Vincenzo's Park Joo-hyung (Song Joong-ki) heads for a fish-shaped bread stall on this street in episode 16, and Yumi (Kim Go-eun) bumps into her ex outside a shop in episode 7 of Yumi's Cells.

The neighboring Pangyoyeok-ro 10, 14 and 18 lanes add more: Open Coffee (판교역로14번길 15) appears in six dramas including Again My Life, Melancholia and Oh My Baby, and Old Stand pub (판교역로10번길 27) shows up in Run On and Dali and the Cocky Prince. Bring a light jacket and plan an hour to browse and sit down for coffee.

Jeongja-dong and Tancheon Stream: Secretary Kim and Something in the Rain

Cafe Helsinki (헬싱키)

One Suin-Bundang or Shinbundang stop north of Pangyo, Jeongja-dong is known for its cafe street. Cafe Helsinki at 경기도 성남시 분당구 정자일로213번길 10 is where Kim Mi-so (Park Min-young) meets a friend in What's Wrong with Secretary Kim, and where Ji Sun-woo (Kim Hee-ae) spends time with her son in The World of the Married. The KBS show VJ Special Forces also featured its meringue latte. The True Beauty apartment exit scene was filmed a few doors away at 정자일로213번길 18.

Jeongja Station and Tancheon Underpass (정자역·탄천 지하보도)

Something in the Rain fans should come here. In episode 9, Yoon Jin-ah (Son Ye-jin) walks out of Exit 1 of Jeongja Station (성남대로 333) on her way home, and in episode 5 she and Seo Jun-hee (Jung Hae-in) walk through the Tancheon underpass at 경기도 성남시 분당구 이매동 144. Tancheon Stream runs the full length of Bundang, with a flat walking and cycling path that makes a calm 20 to 30 minute stroll between Jeongja and Imae stations.

Seohyeon: Shopping, Spicy Galbi-jjim and Idol-Signed Restaurants

Seohyeon Station (경기도 성남시 분당구 성남대로 601) is Bundang's downtown, with the AK Plaza department store above the station and busy food alleys behind it. The station platform appears in episode 10 of Night After Night (밤이면 밤마다), and Cafe on the Desk (서현로237번길 3) is where Gong Woo-jin meets clients in episode 13 of Still 17.

This is the best place in the district for dinner. Kim Cheong-ja Spicy Galbi-jjim (김청자매운갈비찜 서현점, 황새울로330번길 16, 2nd floor) has a signed note from Super Junior's Donghae and has appeared on Shin Dong-yup and Sung Si-kyung's What Shall We Eat Today. Juneu Donkatsu Naengmyeon (서현로210번길 14) was a stop for gugudan members Sejeong and Mina. Near Pangyo, Haenghaeryeong Sujebi (성남대로144번길 14) was featured on Tasty Guys episode 357, and Gamiok (탄천로 181) is the seolleongtang restaurant from the film Punch (완득이).

Yuldong Park: A Lakeside Break from the City

Yuldong Park (경기도 성남시 분당구 문정로 72) wraps around a reservoir on Bundang's eastern edge, with a lakeside walking loop, a book-themed library building, open lawns and a bungee jump tower over the water. In the dataset it is the date spot for 2AM's Jo Kwon and Ga-in on We Got Married. It is a relaxing finish after the office streets, especially at sunset.

From Seohyeon Station, take a local bus heading toward the park or a taxi of about 10 minutes. Wear comfortable shoes for the lakeside loop.

Half-Day Bundang K-Drama Itinerary

Late morning: start at Pangyo Station and walk to U-Space Plaza, the Neowiz rooftop exterior and the Hwarang footbridge for Start-Up and Her Private Life photos.

Lunch: walk back past the station to the Baekhyeon-dong cafe street and Open Coffee for a Still 17 and Vincenzo coffee break.

Afternoon: ride one stop to Jeongja Station, visit Cafe Helsinki, then follow Tancheon Stream toward Imae for the Something in the Rain underpass.

Evening: continue one stop to Seohyeon for spicy galbi-jjim, then take a taxi to Yuldong Park for a sunset lake walk. The Shinbundang Line gets you back to Gangnam in about 20 minutes from Jeongja or Pangyo.

Use a T-money card for every subway and bus in Seoul and Gyeonggi; transfers within the time limit are discounted. Pangyo's office streets are quietest on weekends, which is ideal for photos, but some small cafes close on Sundays, so check Naver Map hours first.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get to Pangyo Techno Valley from Seoul?

Take the Shinbundang Line from Gangnam Station to Pangyo Station, about 15 minutes. U-Space Plaza at 경기도 성남시 분당구 판교역로 233-1 is about a 10 minute walk from the station.

Where was Her Private Life's rooftop apartment filmed?

The KCISA public dataset lists the Neowiz building at 경기도 성남시 분당구 대왕판교로645번길 14 in Pangyo as Seong Deok-mi's rooftop apartment. It is an office building, so view it from the street.

Where is the Something in the Rain underpass?

The dataset places the walk by Yoon Jin-ah and Seo Jun-hee in episode 5 at the Tancheon underpass, 경기도 성남시 분당구 이매동 144, near Imae Station on the Suin-Bundang and Gyeonggang lines.

Is Bundang worth a day trip for K-drama fans?

Yes. Seongnam has 202 filming records in the public dataset, and most of the Bundang spots sit along the Shinbundang and Suin-Bundang lines, so you can see office, cafe and stream locations from many dramas in a half-day.
