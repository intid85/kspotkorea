Why Seocho-gu Is a K-Drama Favorite

Seocho-gu sits on the south bank of the Han River, just west of Gangnam. It has riverside parks and lit-up bridges in the north, Korea's main court district in the middle, and the Seoul Arts Center and quiet stream-side cafe streets around Yangjae in the south.

In the public filming-location dataset from the Korea Culture Information Service Agency (KCISA), Seocho-gu has 295 records. Of those, 162 come from TV dramas, 83 from variety and food shows, 47 from K-Pop artists and 3 from films. By place type, 128 are parks and public spaces, 119 are restaurants and 20 are cafes.

Banpo Bridge and the Seoul Central District Court lead with 28 records each, followed by Some Sevit with 13. If a K-Drama character jogs across a bridge at night or leaves a courthouse after a divorce, it was probably filmed here.

Banpo Bridge, Some Sevit and Seorae Island: The Han River Night Scene

Banpo Bridge and Jamsu Bridge (반포대교 · 잠수교)

Banpo Bridge (서울특별시 서초구 반포동 반포대교) is probably the most filmed bridge in Korea. The KCISA records include Run On episode 6, where Ki Sun-gyeom stops jogging on the bridge to ask himself why he has never loved anyone, and Secret Garden's final episode 20, where Secretary Kim and Min Ah-young find a message in a bottle at the foot of the bridge on a snowy day. In Familiar Wife episode 2, Seo Woo-jin runs here, and in Forecasting Love and Weather episode 4, Jin Ha-kyung jogs across it.

The low deck below it is Jamsu Bridge (잠수교), which you can cross on foot. The dataset lists it as the spot where Yi-seo tosses a coin with her worries into the river in Itaewon Class, and as a photo location for NCT 127's Hello! #SEOUL photo book. The Moonlight Rainbow Fountain on Banpo Bridge usually runs from spring to autumn with several evening shows; check the Seoul Hangang Parks website for this year's schedule.

Some Sevit (세빛섬)

The three glass buildings floating next to the bridge are Some Sevit (서울특별시 서초구 올림픽대로 683). In Goblin episode 12, the confused Grim Reaper meets a fellow reaper here. In Misaeng episode 13, Jang Baek-ki asks Kang Hae-joon out for a drink at night, and in Hwayugi episode 5, Jin Seon-mi and Son Oh-gong come here after chasing away an evil spirit. The promenade is open all day and is free.

Banpo Hangang Park and Seorae Island (반포한강공원 · 서래섬)

Banpo Hangang Park (서울특별시 서초구 신반포로11길 40) is listed as the filming location of BTS's RUN music video, and of the scene in Hotel del Luna where Oh Ji-ho and young Kim Kang-hoon meet the goddess Mago. A ten-minute walk west, Seorae Island (서울특별시 서초구 반포동 137-41) is a small island linked by three footbridges. In Bride of the Water God episode 3, Habaek and Yoon So-ah walk here and sit on the western corner to watch a large outdoor screen.

Getting there: Take Line 3, 7 or 9 to Express Bus Terminal Station and walk about 15 to 20 minutes north to the river, or take Line 9 to Sinbanpo Station.

Seocho-dong Court District: Where Legal Dramas Are Filmed

Seoul Central District Court (서울중앙지방법원)

Korea's legal dramas keep coming back to the Seoul Court Complex (서울특별시 서초구 서초중앙로 157). The dataset says most of the courtroom exteriors in I Can Hear Your Voice were filmed here, and that Miss Hammurabi's judges Park Cha-oh-reum and Im Ba-reun are often seen in front of the east wing. In the final episode of Extraordinary Attorney Woo, Tae Su-mi holds a press conference in front of the building and withdraws as a minister nominee. Hyena, Your Honor, Innocent Defendant and The Penthouse 3 also filmed here.

The grounds are open on weekdays from 9 AM to 6 PM. It is a working court, so take photos only from public areas.

Seoul National University of Education Station (교대역)

The nearest subway stop, Gyodae Station (Lines 2 and 3), is also a location. In Miss Hammurabi episode 1, the two judges first meet as adults at this station, and in episode 4 they help women handing out flyers outside Exit 11. In Judge vs. Judge, Lee Jung-joo gets off at Exit 10 on her way to work. From Exit 10 or 11, the court is about a 10-minute walk.

Boutique Monaco and GT Tower (부띠크모나코 · GT타워)

Near Gangnam Station (Line 2 / Shinbundang Line), two buildings on the Seocho side of the road come from My Love from the Star. GT Tower (서울특별시 서초구 서초대로 411) is where Cheon Song-yi lives in episode 5, and Boutique Monaco (서울특별시 서초구 서초대로 397) is the rooftop where Do Min-joon threatens Lee Jae-kyung in episode 14. Both are private buildings, so view them from the street.

Seoul Arts Center and Yangjae: Concerts, Forest and Cafe Streets

Seoul Arts Center (예술의전당)

The Seoul Arts Center (서울특별시 서초구 남부순환로 2406) is Korea's biggest performing arts complex. In Do You Like Brahms? episode 1, its concert hall is the venue for the university's 60th-anniversary concert where Park Joon-young plays piano. In The King: Eternal Monarch episode 1, Lee Lim arrives in 1994 in front of the art museum here. The outdoor plaza is open all day. Take Line 3 to Nambu Bus Terminal Station, Exit 5, and walk about 10 minutes.

Yangjae Citizen's Forest and Seocho Culture & Arts Park

Yangjae Citizen's Forest Station (서울특별시 서초구 매헌로 116) on the Shinbundang Line appears in Start-Up episode 2, when Seo Dal-mi gets off here. The dataset also lists it as the station from Squid Game where the salesman asks, "Would you like to play a game with me?" and starts the ddakji game. The large, shady Yangjae Citizen's Forest is next to the station.

A short taxi ride away, Seocho Culture & Arts Park (서초문화예술공원, 서울특별시 서초구 바우뫼로12길 40) is where Dong-baek and Yong-sik share a kiss in the final episode of When the Camellia Blooms.

Yangjaecheon cafe street

Along the Yangjae stream, Cafe Lily Blanc (카페 릴리블랑, 서울특별시 서초구 양재천로19길 52) is the cafe used in episodes 1, 3 and 9 of Should We Kiss First and in My Golden Life. It is open weekdays 8 AM to 10 PM and Saturdays 10 AM to 10 PM, and closed Sundays. Around the corner, Miss Yoon (미쓰윤, 서울특별시 서초구 양재천로17길 3) is where TWICE shot part of their YES or YES album, and it also appears in Hwayugi episode 1. It is open weekdays only, 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM.

Where to Eat: TV-Famous Seocho Restaurants

Yeongdong Jokbal (영동족발 본점, 서울특별시 서초구 남부순환로358길 8) near Yangjae Station has appeared on Wednesday Food Talk and is listed as a favorite of Kang Daniel. Close by, Gangchon Wonjo Jjukkumi (강촌원조쭈꾸미본점, 서울특별시 서초구 남부순환로350길 36) has been on Delicious Guys and several evening TV shows for its spicy stir-fried baby octopus. It is open daily from 11 AM to 11:30 PM.

For a lighter meal, Odaesan Sanchae (오대산 산채전문점, 서울특별시 서초구 남부순환로 2640-11 1층) serves a mountain-vegetable set meal with about 30 side dishes and nameul bibimbap. It is open daily from 10 AM to 10 PM. Les Prémices (레프레미스, 서울특별시 서초구 남부순환로337길 36) is the macaron shop BLACKPINK's Rosé praised on Wednesday Food Talk. It is closed on Mondays.

In Jamwon-dong, near Sinsa Station (Line 3), Pro Ganjang Gejang (프로간장게장, 서울특별시 서초구 강남대로97길 9) is famous for soy-marinated raw crab, with signed visits from Super Junior's Heechul, TWICE's Momo and Davichi. Jamwon Tteokbokki (잠원떡볶이, 서울특별시 서초구 잠원로4길 45) is a small snack shop loved by Dynamic Duo's Gaeko and Super Junior's Kyuhyun. Hours change, so check Naver Map before you go.

Half-Day Seocho-gu K-Spot Route

Start in the early afternoon at Nambu Bus Terminal Station and walk to the Seoul Arts Center. Ride Line 3 one stop to Yangjae Station for a late lunch at Odaesan Sanchae or Yeongdong Jokbal, then stroll the Yangjaecheon cafe street to Cafe Lily Blanc.

Next, take Line 3 north to Gyodae Station for the court district from Miss Hammurabi and Extraordinary Attorney Woo, then continue to Express Bus Terminal Station. Walk to the river before sunset, cross Jamsu Bridge on foot, see Some Sevit light up and, in fountain season, watch the Moonlight Rainbow Fountain from Banpo Hangang Park.

With more time, add Gangnam Station for My Love from the Star or Yangjae Citizen's Forest Station for Squid Game. Show taxi drivers the Korean address for each stop; most rides inside Seocho-gu take 10 to 20 minutes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the bridge Ki Sun-gyeom jogs on in Run On?

It is Banpo Bridge (반포대교) in Seocho-gu. The KCISA dataset lists it for Run On episode 6. Take Line 3, 7 or 9 to Express Bus Terminal Station and walk about 15 to 20 minutes to Banpo Hangang Park.

When does the Banpo Bridge Moonlight Rainbow Fountain run?

It usually runs in the warmer months from spring to autumn, with several evening shows. Dates and times change each year, so check the Seoul Hangang Parks website before you go. You can still walk Jamsu Bridge and see Some Sevit lit up outside fountain season.

Can I visit the courthouse from Extraordinary Attorney Woo?

The Seoul Court Complex (서울특별시 서초구 서초중앙로 157) is a working court open on weekdays from 9 AM to 6 PM. You can see the outside from public areas. Do not take photos or videos inside courtrooms.

Where is the Squid Game ddakji station?

The KCISA dataset lists Yangjae Citizen's Forest Station (양재시민의숲역, 서울특별시 서초구 매헌로 116) on the Shinbundang Line for the scene where the salesman offers the ddakji game.

Is Seocho-gu part of Gangnam?

No. Seocho-gu is a separate district just west of Gangnam-gu. The border runs along Gangnam-daero, so Gangnam Station sits on both sides, and GT Tower and Boutique Monaco are on the Seocho side.
