Why Incheon's Jung-gu Is a K-Drama Favorite

Most visitors pass through Incheon's Jung-gu without knowing it. The district covers both the old port neighborhood around Incheon Station and Yeongjong Island, home to Incheon International Airport. That mix of 19th-century treaty-port streets, Chinatown, a seaside promenade, a giant airport and glossy resort hotels gives drama crews almost every kind of backdrop within one district.

In the public filming-location dataset from the Korea Culture Information Service Agency (KCISA), Incheon's Jung-gu has 335 records. Of those, 225 come from TV dramas, 89 from variety and food shows, 20 from K-Pop artists and one from a film. By place type, 174 are parks and public spaces, 87 are restaurants, 45 are hotels and 28 are cafes.

The airport alone has 86 records, Paradise City resort has 24 and Grand Hyatt Incheon has 17. For a walking trip, though, the best part is the old port: Wolmi Theme Park (14 records), Incheon Art Platform (12) and Jayu Park are all within about 15 minutes of Incheon Station, the last stop on Seoul Subway Line 1.

Chinatown and Songwol-dong: Jajangmyeon and Fairy-Tale Murals

Incheon Chinatown (차이나타운로)

Step out of Incheon Station and the red main gate of Chinatown is right across the road. The KCISA records list Chinatown-ro 44beon-gil (인천광역시 중구 차이나타운로44번길) as the street where Wang Joo-hyun takes a selfie at the entrance in Witch Hunter (Night After Night) episode 13, and as the gang territory of the fictional Haewol-dong in Tell Me What You Saw episodes 8, 9 and 11.

Chinatown is also where jajangmyeon, the black-bean noodle dish every K-Drama character orders on moving day, became a Korean classic. The Jajangmyeon Museum (짜장면박물관, 인천광역시 중구 차이나타운로 56-14) is housed in the old Gonghwachun restaurant building. In A Korean Odyssey, the grandson of the general store owner sets up an unusual street stall in front of it. The dataset lists it as open Tuesday to Sunday from 9 AM to 6 PM and closed Mondays.

Songwol-dong Fairy Tale Village (송월동 동화마을)

A five-minute walk north of Chinatown, Songwol-dong's alleys are painted with scenes from fairy tales like The Wizard of Oz and Alice in Wonderland. It is mostly a photo stop, but the dataset includes Cafe Oz (카페오즈, 인천광역시 중구 동화마을길 21-19), featured on three TV shows for its jajang bingsu, a shaved-ice dessert styled like black-bean noodles. It is open daily from 11:30 AM to 6:30 PM.

Jayu Park and Incheon Art Platform: Goblin's Port Town

Jayu Park (인천자유공원)

Jayu Park (Freedom Park), Korea's first Western-style park, sits on a hill above Chinatown at 인천광역시 중구 송학동1가 1-1. The KCISA dataset says Kim Shin and Ji Eun-tak come here for a walk in Goblin episode 8, and another record calls it the place where Eun-tak blows out a flame to summon Kim Shin. In the final episode 12 of Business Proposal, Kang Tae-moo proposes to Shin Ha-ri on a park path. Love Alarm episode 1 shows Lee Hye-young and Hwang Sun-oh cycling past on the way to school, and School 2021 episode 14 and Model Detective also filmed here. The park is open all day. Walk up the stairs behind Chinatown, about 15 minutes from Incheon Station, for views over the harbor.

Incheon Art Platform (인천아트플랫폼)

Down the hill toward the port, Incheon Art Platform (인천아트플랫폼, 인천광역시 중구 제물량로218번길 3) is a cluster of red-brick warehouses turned into studios and galleries. In Goblin episode 14, Kim Shin walks here after meeting Eun-tak at the Hanmi Bookstore, which is a short taxi ride away in Dong-gu. It also played the Kirin Art School in Dream High, and in Business Proposal episode 2 Jin Young-seo and Kang Tae-moo meet here to talk. Monthly Magazine Home, Doom at Your Service and Switch also filmed here. The dataset lists it as open Tuesday to Sunday from 9 AM to 6 PM and closed Mondays, but the outdoor plaza is easy to walk through anytime.

Wolmido: Seaside Rides, Lighthouse and Squid Game

Wolmi Theme Park and My Land (월미테마파크 · 마이랜드)

Wolmido is a small former island west of Incheon Station, linked by the elevated Wolmi Sea Train. Wolmi Theme Park (인천광역시 중구 월미문화로 81) has 14 records. In Touch Your Heart episode 11, Kwon Jung-rok takes Oh Yoon-seo here on a date and promises to protect her no matter what. In Let's Fight Ghost episodes 7 and 8, Bong-pal finally keeps his promise to take Hyun-ji to an amusement park. The dataset also notes Rain's and Jay Park's Gang music videos and a TWICE ride on the Disco Pang Pang. It is open from 10 AM to 9 PM on weekdays and until 11 PM on weekends.

Next door, My Land (인천광역시 중구 월미로234번길 7) is where gangster Jang Deok-su meets an acquaintance after the games stop in Squid Game, and Extracurricular episodes 5 and 6 also filmed key scenes here.

Wolmi Culture Street and the lighthouse (월미문화의거리)

The seaside promenade, Wolmi Culture Street (인천광역시 중구 북성동1가 98-57), is open all day. In Stove League episode 13, Baek Seung-soo asks Im Dong-gyu to rejoin the Dreams at the Wolmido lighthouse at the end of the street. In Twenty-Five Twenty-One episode 10, Moon Ji-woong hangs out with breakdancers here, and in Lawless Lawyer episode 4 Ha Jae-yi and Bong Sang-pil walk along the water. Be Melodramatic and Switch also filmed here. Come at sunset, when the sky over the Yellow Sea turns orange.

Yeongjong Island: The Airport and Resort Hotels

Incheon International Airport (인천국제공항, 인천광역시 중구 공항로 272) is the most filmed single place in this district. The dataset lists scenes from SKY Castle episode 11, when Cha Se-ri arrives from the United States, Goong episode 2, when Prince Lee Yul lands from England, and What's Wrong with Secretary Kim episode 13 in Terminal 2. If you fly into Korea, you have already seen it.

From Terminal 1, a free shuttle runs to Paradise City (파라다이스시티, 인천광역시 중구 영종해안남로321번길 186), the resort that played Lee Gon's hotel in The King: Eternal Monarch, the Royal Pacific hotel in Hotel del Luna episode 1 and a Granada hotel in Memories of the Alhambra. Grand Hyatt Incheon (인천광역시 중구 영종해안남로321번길 208) next door appears in Itaewon Class episode 10, Vincenzo episode 14 and Mine. If you have a long layover, both are easy to visit.

Where to Eat: TV-Famous Incheon Restaurants

England Donkkaseu (잉글랜드돈까스, 인천광역시 중구 우현로90번길 7 혜성빌딩 2층) is an old-style Western restaurant. In Reply 1988, Jung-hwan's whole family eats out here, and in Navillera episode 6 Shim Deok-chul and Choi Hae-nam celebrate their wedding anniversary. It is open Tuesday to Sunday from 11:30 AM to 8:30 PM and closed Mondays.

In Chinatown, Shinseung Banjeom (신승반점, 인천광역시 중구 차이나타운로44번길 31-3) and Mandabok (만다복, 인천광역시 중구 차이나타운로 36) both appeared on Wednesday Food Talk for their jajangmyeon, and both are listed as open daily from 11 AM to 9 PM. For a snack, Incheondang (인천당, 인천광역시 중구 참외전로 138) sells traditional Korean sweets and has been on five TV shows. Near Sinpo Market, Myeongwol-jip (명월집, 인천광역시 중구 신포로23번길 41) serves kimchi-jjigae set meals and is closed on Sundays. Ilgwang Jeongu Lighthouse (일광전구 라이트하우스, 인천광역시 중구 참외전로174번길 8-1), a cafe in an old light-bulb factory, appears in It's Okay to Not Be Okay episode 1 and Kkondae Intern. Half-Day and One-Day Incheon Jung-gu K-Spot Routes

For a half day, take Line 1 or the Suin-Bundang Line to Incheon Station, about 60 to 70 minutes from Seoul Station. Walk through the Chinatown gate, eat jajangmyeon for an early lunch, visit the Jajangmyeon Museum and the Songwol-dong murals, then climb to Jayu Park for the Goblin and Business Proposal walk. Head downhill to Incheon Art Platform and finish with tonkatsu at England Donkkaseu.

For a full day, take the Wolmi Sea Train from Incheon Station in the afternoon. Ride the rides at Wolmi Theme Park, then walk Wolmi Culture Street to the lighthouse from Stove League for sunset. If you are flying out, save Yeongjong Island for your last day and stop at Paradise City before your flight.

Show taxi drivers the Korean address for each stop. A taxi from Incheon Station to Wolmido takes about 10 minutes, and the airport is 40 to 50 minutes away by car or AREX.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where did Goblin film in Incheon?

According to the KCISA public dataset, Goblin filmed at Jayu Park (인천광역시 중구 송학동1가 1-1) in episode 8 and at Incheon Art Platform (인천광역시 중구 제물량로218번길 3) in episode 14. Both are within a 15-minute walk of Incheon Station.

Where did Business Proposal film the proposal scene?

The dataset says Kang Tae-moo proposes to Shin Ha-ri on a path in Jayu Park (인천자유공원), Jung-gu, Incheon, in the final episode 12.

How do I get from Seoul to Incheon Chinatown?

Take Seoul Subway Line 1 (Incheon-bound) or the Suin-Bundang Line to Incheon Station, the last stop. The Chinatown gate is directly across the street. The trip from Seoul Station takes about 60 to 70 minutes.

Where is the amusement park from Touch Your Heart?

It is Wolmi Theme Park (월미테마파크), 인천광역시 중구 월미문화로 81, on Wolmido. Take the Wolmi Sea Train from Incheon Station.

Which Squid Game location is in Incheon's Jung-gu?

The KCISA dataset lists My Land (마이랜드), 인천광역시 중구 월미로234번길 7 on Wolmido, where Jang Deok-su meets an acquaintance after the games stop.
