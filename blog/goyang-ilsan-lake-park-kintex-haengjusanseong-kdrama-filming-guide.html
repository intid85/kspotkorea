Goyang, the city just northwest of Seoul that includes Ilsan, has 516 records in Korea's public KCISA filming-location dataset, and 387 of them come from TV dramas. With two broadcaster production centers nearby, crews use its huge lake park, wide plazas, exhibition halls and riverside fortress again and again. This guide links Ilsan Lake Park, the Jeongbalsan Station area, KINTEX and Haengjusanseong into an easy subway day trip with Korean addresses for taxis.

Why Goyang and Ilsan Keep Showing Up in K-Dramas

Goyang is the big city just northwest of Seoul, and most visitors know its newer half, Ilsan. If you have watched Korean TV for a few years, you have almost certainly seen it. Two of Korea's largest broadcasters run production centers here, MBC Dream Center and SBS Ilsan Production Center, so crews do not have to travel far when a script calls for a lakeside walk, a wide plaza or a quiet restaurant room. Ilsan was planned in the early 1990s, which gives it broad tree-lined streets, a huge man-made lake and modern buildings that can stand in for almost anywhere.

In the public filming-location dataset from the Korea Culture Information Service Agency (KCISA), Goyang-si has 516 records. Of those, 387 come from TV dramas, 91 from variety and food shows, 35 from K-Pop artists and three from films. By place type, 229 are parks and public spaces and 210 are restaurants, with 39 cafes. Most entries sit in Ilsandong-gu (about 340), followed by Deogyang-gu (95) and Ilsanseo-gu (about 80).

The single most filmed place is Ilsan Lake Park with 49 records. Asoan, a Korean restaurant a short walk away, has 45, and Ilsan Culture Square has 17. That makes a Goyang trip easy to plan: one subway station, Jeongbalsan on Line 3, puts you within walking distance of all three.

Ilsan Lake Park: The Metasequoia Path and Lakeside Dates

Ilsan Lake Park (일산호수공원)

Ilsan Lake Park, at 경기도 고양시 일산동구 호수로 731, wraps around a man-made lake with walking and cycling paths, a rose garden and a well-known metasequoia lane. The KCISA records read like a list of K-Drama classics. In The Heirs episode 11, Cha Eun-sang and Kim Tan walk the metasequoia path among autumn leaves the morning after a camping trip. In Encounter (also known as Boyfriend) episode 11, Cha Soo-hyun and Kim Jin-hyuk head to the same lane on a date, where he shows her how to take photos and later gives her a couple ring. In Touch Your Heart episode 7, Kwon Jung-rok hands Oh Yoon-seo a handwritten letter on the path, and Hyde Jekyll, Me episode 12 has Hyun Bin and Han Ji-min walking there too.

The list keeps going. In My Love from the Star episode 17, Cheon Song-yi and Do Min-joon argue in the park, and in episode 21 Song-yi is shooting a drama at the park's waterfall plaza. In Tale of the Nine-Tailed episode 1, Lee Yeon sits on a bench and watches families pass. In Yumi's Cells, Kim Yu-mi sets up a meeting by the fountain in the rose garden during the Goyang flower festival, and in Yumi's Cells 2 episode 1 she jogs along the lake. A Gentleman's Dignity and Hotel del Luna also filmed here.

The dataset lists the park as open daily from 8 AM to 8 PM. From Jeongbalsan Station (Line 3), walk about 10 minutes toward the lake. Late October and early November bring the best metasequoia color.

Jeongbalsan Station Area: Asoan, Culture Square and La Festa

Asoan Ilsan main branch (아소산 일산본점)

Asoan, at 경기도 고양시 일산동구 정발산로 128, is the place K-Drama writers send powerful people to make secret deals. It has 45 records in the dataset. In SKY Castle episode 10, Kang Joon-sang meets his neighbor Cha Min-hyuk here to talk about the children's education. In The World of the Married episodes 9 and 10, Yeo Byung-gyu meets Dr. Kim Yoon-ki here, and in Itaewon Class episode 14 Kang Min-jung and Jo Yi-seo meet an investor. The film Inside Men used it too. The dataset lists it as open daily from 11:30 AM to 9:30 PM.

Ilsan Culture Square (일산문화광장)

Right next to Jeongbalsan Station, Ilsan Culture Square (경기도 고양시 일산동구 중앙로 1271-4) is an open plaza with benches that is open all day. In While You Were Sleeping episode 3, Nam Hong-joo walks through it and tells a little girl who lost her balloon that a tall man will soon help her. In Start-Up, Seo Dal-mi sits on a bench here and calls her father after a fight with her sister. The Heirs also filmed a snowy walk here.

La Festa (라페스타)

Across the street, La Festa (경기도 고양시 일산동구 중앙로 1305-56) is a car-free shopping street with an arched entrance. The KCISA records say the whole cast of Dream High filmed the flash mob in the final episode here. It also appears in How to Hate You episode 1. Shop hours vary by store.

Ilsanseo-gu: KINTEX, One Mount and Aqua Planet

KINTEX (킨텍스)

KINTEX, Korea's largest exhibition center at 경기도 고양시 일산서구 킨텍스로 217-60, is a favorite airport double. The KCISA dataset says Squid Game uses it in the final episode 9, when Gi-hun heads to the airport for Los Angeles. In Now, We Are Breaking Up episode 15, it stands in for Incheon Airport as Yoon Jae-kook leaves and Ha Young-eun comes to say goodbye. Opening times depend on the exhibition schedule. Take the GTX-A express to Kintex Station, or Line 3 to Daehwa Station and walk or take a short bus ride.

One Mount and Aqua Planet Ilsan

A few minutes' walk from KINTEX, One Mount (원마운트, 경기도 고양시 일산서구 한류월드로 300) is a shopping and leisure complex with a snow park. In Love Alarm episode 1, Kim Jo-jo skates there with her two best friends, and in episode 4 she returns for a skating date with Hwang Sun-oh. The dataset also notes that Gain appears here in PSY's Gentleman music video, and Pinocchio episodes 7 and 8 filmed the brothers' reunion here.

Next door, Aqua Planet Ilsan (아쿠아플라넷 일산, 경기도 고양시 일산서구 한류월드로 282) is listed as open daily from 10 AM to 6 PM. In Move to Heaven episode 1, Han Geu-ru visits the aquarium, where his friend Yoon Na-mu works. Kill Me, Heal Me and The Devil Judge also filmed here, and it makes a good rainy-day stop.

Deogyang-gu: Haengjusanseong Fortress and the Han River

Goyang's older half, Deogyang-gu, sits along the Han River. Haengjusanseong (행주산성), the hilltop fortress where Joseon forces won a famous battle in 1593, is now a park with river views. The KCISA dataset lists it at 경기도 고양시 덕양구 행주외동 140-8. In The Veil episode 6, Han Ji-hyuk and Lin Wei come to a memorial stone in the park. In Sell Your Haunted House episode 2, Oh In-beom and Heo Ji-cheol drink morning coffee here at sunrise, and A Piece of Your Mind also filmed here. Take a taxi from Hwajeong Station (Line 3), about 15 minutes, or check KakaoMap for local buses.

Below the fortress, the riverside road has more drama spots. Opera Divas (오페라 디바스, 경기도 고양시 덕양구 행주산성로 141) is where Hong Dae-young has lunch with his twins in 18 Again episode 1, and Ha Young-eun turns down a magazine interview there in Now, We Are Breaking Up episode 13. The dataset adds that Loco and Hwasa's song Don't Give It to Me was born here. The Ts Coffee Factory (더츠커피팩토리, 경기도 고양시 덕양구 행주산성로120번길 8) appears in Signal, Cinderella and Four Knights and Perfect Wife.

Wondang horse ranch (원당종마목장)

Further inland, the Wondang horse ranch (경기도 고양시 덕양구 서삼릉길 233-112) has open pastures and a tree-lined road. It appears in Goong episode 16, That Winter, the Wind Blows episode 5, The World of the Married episode 15 and Secret Garden episode 7, where Kim Joo-won and Gil Ra-im drive down the road. The dataset lists it as open Wednesday to Sunday from 9 AM to 5 PM and closed Mondays and Tuesdays. It is next to the Seosamneung royal tombs, so take a taxi from Samsong Station (Line 3).

Where to Eat: Drama and TV-Famous Goyang Restaurants

Near Haengjusanseong, Gananan Guksu (가나안국수, 경기도 고양시 덕양구 행주로15번길 16) is known for chilled chogye noodles and has been featured on four food shows, including 2TV Saengsaeng Information. The dataset lists it as open daily from 9 AM to 9 PM. Neunggok Halmeoni Bugeotang (능곡할머니북어탕, 경기도 고양시 덕양구 토당로 66-15) serves dried pollack soup and appeared on Amazing Saturday and Delicious Guys. It is open Tuesday to Sunday from 7 AM to 3 PM, so go for breakfast or an early lunch.

In Ilsan, Pino (경기도 고양시 일산동구 애니골길 72) on the Annigol cafe street has 20 drama records, including A Gentleman's Dignity, Jealousy Incarnate episode 18, Itaewon Class episode 6 and Yumi's Cells 2 episode 6. Nearby Baengma Hwasarang (백마 화사랑, 경기도 고양시 일산동구 애니골길 14-14) is the cafe from Another Miss Oh and Signal episode 4. Shilla Myeonok (신라면옥 본점, 경기도 고양시 일산동구 탄중로431번길 15), a cold-noodle restaurant that appears in Stranger, is closed on Mondays and Thursdays. Always check current hours on Naver Map before you go.

Half-Day and One-Day Goyang K-Spot Routes

For a half day, take Line 3 to Jeongbalsan Station. Start at Ilsan Culture Square and La Festa, then walk to Ilsan Lake Park and follow the shore to the metasequoia path and the rose garden. Finish with a late lunch at Asoan,.

For a full day, add Ilsanseo-gu in the afternoon: take a bus or taxi to One Mount and Aqua Planet, then walk to KINTEX for the "airport" building from Squid Game. If you prefer history and river views, spend the morning at Haengjusanseong with chogye noodles at Gananan Guksu instead, then ride Line 3 north to Jeongbalsan for the lake park at sunset.

Show your taxi driver the Korean address for each stop. Allow 25 to 40 minutes by taxi between Haengjusanseong and Ilsan, and about 10 minutes between Jeongbalsan and KINTEX.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the metasequoia path from The Heirs and Encounter?

It is inside Ilsan Lake Park (일산호수공원), 경기도 고양시 일산동구 호수로 731, in Goyang. Walk about 10 minutes from Jeongbalsan Station on Seoul Subway Line 3.

Which airport scene in Squid Game was filmed in Goyang?

According to the KCISA public dataset, the airport scene in Squid Game episode 9 was filmed at KINTEX (킨텍스), 경기도 고양시 일산서구 킨텍스로 217-60. Now, We Are Breaking Up also used it as an airport.

How do I get from Seoul to Ilsan Lake Park?

Take Seoul Subway Line 3 toward Daehwa and get off at Jeongbalsan Station. The trip from central Seoul takes about 50 to 60 minutes.

Where is the restaurant from SKY Castle and The World of the Married?

It is Asoan Ilsan main branch (아소산 일산본점), 경기도 고양시 일산동구 정발산로 128, near Jeongbalsan Station. The dataset lists it as open daily from 11:30 AM to 9:30 PM.

Can I visit the Wondang horse ranch from Secret Garden?

Yes. The dataset lists the Wondang horse ranch (원당종마목장), 경기도 고양시 덕양구 서삼릉길 233-112, as open Wednesday to Sunday from 9 AM to 5 PM. Take a taxi from Samsong Station on Line 3.
