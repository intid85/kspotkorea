Jung-gu, the small district at the very center of Seoul, holds 601 records in Korea's public KCISA filming-location dataset, and 388 of them come from TV dramas. It is the best place in the city to see drama scenes on foot: a stone-wall lane, two historic cathedrals, an elevated garden walk and a futuristic design plaza, all within a few subway stops. This guide links them into one easy walking day with Korean addresses for taxis.

Why Jung-gu Is Seoul's Best K-Drama Walking District

Jung-gu is the old commercial heart of Seoul, squeezed between the palaces of Jongno to the north and Namsan Mountain to the south. It is small, flat in most places and extremely well connected, which is exactly why location managers love it. In the public filming-location dataset published by the Korea Culture Information Service Agency (KCISA), Jung-gu has 601 records.

The mix tells you what kind of trip this is. Of those 601 records, 388 come from TV dramas, 146 from variety and food shows, 56 from K-Pop artists and 11 from films. By place type, 323 are streets, parks and public spaces, while 197 are restaurants. In other words, Jung-gu is less about sitting in an idol's favorite cafe and more about walking the same streets and stairways the characters walked.

Works that appear most often in Jung-gu

The drama list is long and covers almost every era of K-Drama: Goblin, My Love from the Star, Misaeng, Extraordinary Attorney Woo, Vincenzo, Fight for My Way, My Mister, Twenty-Five Twenty-One and True Beauty. Among music entries BTS leads with seven records, followed by BLACKPINK with five. Films include 1987, The Priests, Assassination, The Handmaiden and Veteran. Namsan Tower and the Samsoon stairs are also in Jung-gu, but we covered them in our Itaewon and Namsan guide, so this article stays in the flat downtown.

Deoksugung Stonewall Road and Jeong-dong

If you only have one hour, spend it on Deoksugung Stonewall Road (덕수궁 돌담길), the lane that follows the curved stone wall of Deoksugung Palace from City Hall toward Jeong-dong. It is the single most filmed spot in central Jung-gu after the State Tower Namsan office building, with 29 records in the dataset.

The KCISA records list Goblin, Misaeng, My Love from the Star, Extraordinary Attorney Woo, My Liberation Notes, Brahms, Run On, The Lovers of Prague and Personal Taste, plus a BTS entry. In Personal Taste, Lee Min-ho's character carries a tipsy Son Ye-jin home along the wall in episode 3,, and in My Unfamiliar Family two characters revisit the lane in episode 10 to relive their autumn memories from their twenties. It is open all day, every day, and is at its best from late October to mid-November when the ginkgo trees turn gold.

Keep walking into Jeong-dong

Follow the wall west and it becomes Jeongdong-gil (정동길), which the dataset ties to My Liberation Notes and the film Sunny. Along the way you pass the Seoul Museum of Art (서울시립미술관, 덕수궁길 61), used in Goong (Princess Hours), and the small cafe Le Pool (르풀, 정동길 33), which appears in the dramas Alone With You and Liar Game. Deoksugung Palace itself charges a small entrance fee and closes on Mondays.

Myeongdong: The Cathedral from 1987 and The Priests

From City Hall it is a ten-minute walk east to Myeongdong. Most visitors come for skincare shops and street food, but the hill at the top of the shopping street holds Myeongdong Cathedral (명동대성당, 명동길 74), Korea's first Gothic-style brick church.

The dataset records the cathedral in the film 1987, where it is the place a priest reads out the statement that helped spark the June Democratic Struggle, and in The Priests, where Gang Dong-won's character comes to collect holy relics. The drama Black Knight also filmed here. The cathedral is an active church, so keep voices low and avoid photographing people during mass.

The Myeongdong shopping street

The main Myeongdong shopping street (명동길) appears in True Beauty, Dalja's Spring, She Would Never Know and At a Distance, Spring Is Green, where Bae In-hyuk's character works a part-time job at the crossroads in episode 1. The small MOS Burger on Myeongdong 9-gil (명동9길 37) is where An Eun-jin's character works in More Than Friends, also listed in the dataset with a Lovelyz entry.

Seoul Station, Seoullo 7017 and Yakhyeon Church

Ride one stop from City Hall to Seoul Station and you reach three more strong spots within a short walk of each other.

Seoullo 7017 (서울로7017, 청파로 432) is a former highway overpass turned into an elevated garden walkway. The dataset lists Vincenzo, Fight for My Way, Police University, Little Women, Inspector Koo and Live Up to Your Name, where Kim Nam-gil's Joseon doctor lands in 2017 Seoul on this very bridge. In Police University episode 7, Jung Soo-jung and Jin Young sit in the garden to talk after a court hearing.

Yakhyeon Catholic Church

At the far end of Seoullo, down the stairs toward Jungnim-dong, stands Yakhyeon Catholic Church (중림동 약현성당, 청파로 447-1), the oldest Western-style church building in Korea. It has 27 records, more than the Myeongdong Cathedral, including My Girlfriend Is a Gumiho, where Lee Seung-gi finally confesses to Shin Min-ah in front of the church in episode 11, as well as Her Private Life, The K2, Black Knight, The Fiery Priest, Penthouse 3, Dear My Friends and Iris.

Back beside the station, Culture Station Seoul 284 (문화역서울284, 통일로 1), the restored 1925 station building, appears in Assassination, The Handmaiden, Stairway to Heaven and Inspector Koo. It is now an exhibition space, open Tuesday to Sunday.

Euljiro and Dongdaemun Design Plaza

Euljiro is Seoul's old printing and lighting district, now packed with retro bars and hidden cafes. The dataset ties Daelim Sangga (대림상가, 을지로 157) to Stranger and True Beauty, and the Cheonggye Sangga arcade (청계천로 160) to Vincenzo and Move to Heaven. Paro Grand (파로그랜드, 을지로5길 26), a restaurant near Euljiro, shows up in ten dramas including My Love from the Star, SKY Castle, 18 Again and Weightlifting Fairy Kim Bok-joo.

DDP: Seoul's spaceship

The curved silver Dongdaemun Design Plaza (DDP, 을지로 281) is listed under its operator, the Seoul Design Foundation, with 12 dramas. Black Sun uses it for a meeting that ends in a shooting in episode 5, Are You Human? stages a self-driving car launch here in episode 3, and Vincenzo, The Devil Judge, Melancholia, Love Alarm and Producer all film around its plazas. The buildings are open daily from 10:00 to 20:00 and the LED rose garden and outdoor walkways are free.

Where to Eat: Idol-Approved Jung-gu Restaurants

Jung-gu's 197 restaurant records are dominated by food shows such as Delicious Guys, Baek Jong-won's Top 3 Chef King and Wednesday Food Talk, so you can eat well almost anywhere. For K-Pop fans the clear pick is Geumdwaeji Sikdang (금돼지식당, 다산로 149) near Yaksu Station, a pork-belly restaurant that the dataset links to BTS, TWICE, SHINee, TVXQ, Dynamic Duo and Davichi. It opens daily from 11:30 to 22:00, and queues are long at dinner, so arrive close to opening.

For a quieter coffee, the cafe Uioeui Johap (의외의조합, 동호로17길 121) was used as Kim Mi-so's home in What's Wrong with Secretary Kim and also appears in Nevertheless and Was It Love?. It opens from 12:00 to 18:00.

Half-Day and One-Day Jung-gu K-Spot Routes

For a half day, start at City Hall Station Exit 2, walk Deoksugung Stonewall Road and Jeongdong-gil, then cross to Myeongdong Cathedral and finish with street food on the Myeongdong shopping street. This loop is about 3 kilometers and needs no transport.

For a full day, add Seoul Station in the morning: walk Seoullo 7017 to Yakhyeon Church, come back past Culture Station Seoul 284, then take Line 1 or Line 2 to City Hall for the stone-wall walk and Myeongdong. In the evening, ride Line 2 or Line 4 to Dongdaemun History & Culture Park Station to see DDP lit up, and finish with a late pork-belly dinner at Geumdwaeji Sikdang, one stop away on Line 6 at Yaksu.

Practical tips

Every spot here is on the subway, so a T-money card is all you need. Show the Korean address on your phone to a taxi driver if you get tired, since most rides inside Jung-gu are short. Churches and Deoksugung Palace keep their own hours, and Culture Station Seoul 284 closes on Mondays, so the best day to do this route is Tuesday through Saturday. Myeongdong and Seoul Station also have many mid-range hotels, which makes Jung-gu a convenient base for the rest of your Korea trip.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the Deoksugung stone wall road from Goblin and Extraordinary Attorney Woo?

Deoksugung Stonewall Road starts next to Deoksugung Palace at 서울특별시 중구 세종대로 99, a one-minute walk from City Hall Station Exit 2 on Lines 1 and 2. The lane is open 24 hours a day and is free.

Which Seoul cathedral appears in the movie 1987?

Myeongdong Cathedral at 서울특별시 중구 명동길 74 is the cathedral recorded for 1987 and The Priests in the KCISA public dataset. It is a short walk from Myeongdong Station (Line 4) or Euljiro 1-ga Station (Line 2).

Where did Vincenzo and Fight for My Way film in central Seoul?

Both dramas are recorded at Seoullo 7017, the elevated garden walkway beside Seoul Station at 서울특별시 중구 청파로 432. Vincenzo also filmed at DDP and the Cheonggye Sangga arcade in Jung-gu.

Is Jung-gu a good area to stay for K-Drama fans?

Yes. Myeongdong and Seoul Station have many hotels, and you can walk to the stone-wall road, both cathedrals and Seoullo 7017. Jongno's palaces, Hongdae and Gangnam are all reachable by subway in 10 to 30 minutes.
