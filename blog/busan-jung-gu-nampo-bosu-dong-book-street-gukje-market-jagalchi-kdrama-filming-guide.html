Busan Jung-gu (Nampo, Bosu-dong Book Street, Gukje Market, Jagalchi): K-Drama & Movie Filming Spots Guide

Busan's Jung-gu has 76 records in Korea's public KCISA filming-location dataset, from Bosu-dong Book Street in The Attorney and Shopping King Louie to Hwaguk Banjeom in Confidential Assignment and the Commodore Hotel in Crash Landing on You. This guide links the book street, Gukje and Kkangtong markets, BIFF Square and Jagalchi into one walkable Line 1 half day.

Why Busan Jung-gu Is the Old-Downtown Screen Set

Before Haeundae's towers and Gwangalli's bridge, Busan's downtown was Jung-gu, the small harbor district around Nampo-dong, Gwangbok-dong and Bupyeong-dong. It is where the city's markets, cinemas and port grew up, and it still feels dense, busy and very Busan. Korea's public KCISA filming-location dataset lists 76 records with a Jung-gu address: 52 from variety and food shows, 15 from idol and artist visits, 5 from films and 4 from TV dramas.

The screen highlights are compact. Bosu-dong Book Street leads with 6 records, including the films The Attorney and The King and the dramas Shopping King Louie. Hwaguk Banjeom, a Chinese restaurant, appears in Confidential Assignment, The King: Eternal Monarch and Omniscient Interfering View. The Commodore Hotel Busan stood in for a Pyongyang hotel in Crash Landing on You. Almost everything else is food: Jung-gu is the most food-show-heavy district we have covered, with fish cake shops, milmyeon noodle houses and market stalls that Baek Jong-won, Running Man and Wednesday Food Talk all visited.

Everything in this guide sits within roughly a 20 minute walk of Nampo and Jagalchi stations on Busan Metro Line 1, so it is an easy half day with no transfers.

Bosu-dong Book Street: The Attorney, The King and Shopping King Louie

Bosu-dong Book Street (보수동 책방골목, 부산광역시 중구 대청로 67-1) is a narrow lane of second-hand bookshops with books stacked floor to ceiling, a market that started after the Korean War. In The Attorney, Song Kang-ho's character comes back here to buy back the bar-exam books he once sold to a used bookstore. In the opening of the film The King, Jo In-sung runs through the alley, and in episode 8 of Shopping King Louie, Louie (Seo In-guk) and Go Bok-sil (Nam Ji-hyun) share their kiss in the bookshop lane, right after Bok-sil discovers that Louie can read Japanese, French, German and Spanish as he flips through books.

The alley also turns up in dating show Heart Signal, where Oh Young-ju and Lee Kyu-bin gave each other books here, and in visits by Girl's Day's Minah and three members of THE BOYZ (Jacob, Hyunjae and Juyeon). The dataset lists the lane as open around the clock, but most shops keep daytime hours and many close on Sundays, so visit on a weekday afternoon.

Getting there

From Jagalchi Station (Line 1), walk north across Gukje Market and Bupyeong Market, about 12 to 15 minutes. For a taxi, show 보수동 책방골목 or 대청로 67-1.

Gukje Market and Kkangtong Market: Running Man, Taeyang and Fish Cake Alley

Gukje Market (국제시장, 국제시장 2길 25) is the sprawling covered market that gave the 2014 film Ode to My Father its Korean title. In the dataset, Running Man episode 421 staged a surprise chase through its lanes, fromis_9's Jiwon and Chaeyoung did a market food tour here, and Taeyang visited Kkotbunine (꽃분이네, 중구로 42), the small goods shop made famous by that film. The market runs daily from 9am to 8pm and closes on the 1st and 3rd Monday.

Just to the north, Bupyeong Kkangtong Market (부평깡통시장, 부평1길 48) is where Taeyang ate street food on TV. Its lanes are the fish cake capital of the district: VJ Special Forces visited more than ten eomuk shops here, including Hwangong Eomuk (환공어묵, 중구로43번길 25) and Hyoseong Eomuk (효성어묵 부평점, 중구로43번길 39), both open from early morning. Igane Tteokbokki (이가네떡볶이, 부평1길 48) appears on both Baek Jong-won's Top 3 Chef King and VJ Special Forces, open Monday to Saturday until 5pm.

The night market (야시장) opens daily from 7:30pm to 11:30pm, according to the dataset. Nearby, Wonjo Kkangtong Golmok Bibim Dangmyeon (원조 깡통골목 비빔당면, 중구로47번길 28), a spicy cold glass-noodle stall, has been on Top 3 Chef King, Live Today Morning and Korean Table. Gukje Tongdak (국제통닭 부평본점, 중구로 47번길 33-1) is the fried chicken shop fromis_9's Saerom visited in her Busan vlog.

Getting there

Use Jagalchi Station (Line 1) Exit 7 for Gukje Market, about a 5 minute walk; Kkangtong Market is a further 5 minutes north.

BIFF Square and Gwangbok-ro: The King: Eternal Monarch and Street Snacks

BIFF Square (BIFF광장) is the cinema street that hosted the first Busan International Film Festival, with celebrity handprints set into the pavement. It is also the best place to try ssiat hotteok, a sweet pancake filled with seeds and nuts. Nampo-dong Wonjo Chapssal Hotteok (남포동 원조 찹쌀호떡 본점, 비프광장로 36), featured on Live Today Morning, opens daily from 10am to 9pm.

A block east, the Gwangbok-ro fashion street (광복로 패션거리) is where Kang Shin-jae walks along with an ice cream in The King: Eternal Monarch. For a sit-down meal, Wonsan Myeonok (원산면옥, 광복로 56-8) is a Hamheung-style naengmyeon house praised on Wednesday Food Talk, and Ilmi Danpatjuk (일미단팥죽, 광복로49번길 11) serves sweet red-bean porridge from Top 3 Chef King. Kang Daniel recommended Nampo Sujebi (남포수제비, 광복로49번길 7-1) for hand-torn noodle soup and Ijaemo Pizza (이재모피자, 광복중앙로 31), a local pizza institution closed on Sundays.

Getting there

Nampo Station (Line 1) Exit 1 or Jagalchi Station Exit 7 both put you within a 3 minute walk.

Jagalchi Market, Hwaguk Banjeom and the Commodore Hotel

Jagalchi Market (자갈치시장, 자갈치해안로 52) is Korea's best-known fish market, facing the harbor. In 2 Days & 1 Night season 1 the cast helped out by delivering ice here, and April filmed an episode of their web show at the market. Buy seafood downstairs and have it prepared upstairs, or try a dataset pick: Baekhwa Yanggopchang 1-ho (백화양곱창1호, 자갈치로 23) for grilled beef tripe, Gimhae Sikdang (김해식당, 자갈치로 51-2) for monkfish, and Juyeonne Gomjangeo (주연네곰장어, 자갈치해안로 61) for grilled hagfish from Battle Trip. The market is open daily from 5am to 10pm, closing on the 1st and 3rd Tuesday.

Hwaguk Banjeom

Hwaguk Banjeom (화국반점, 백산길 3) is the Chinese restaurant where Hyun Bin jumps out of a window in Confidential Assignment, and where Myeong-ho's birthday party is held in The King: Eternal Monarch. Omniscient Interfering View episode 22 ordered its fried shrimp and ganjjajang. It opens daily from 11:30am to 9:30pm, closing on the 1st and 3rd Monday.

Commodore Hotel Busan

The Commodore Hotel Busan (코모도호텔 부산, 중구로 151), with its traditional palace-style roof, played the upscale Pyongyang hotel the "Dooly couple" visit in Crash Landing on You, and was a main setting of the film Real. It sits on the hill above Jungang Station (Line 1), a short taxi ride from Nampo.

A Half-Day Jung-gu Itinerary

Start around 11am at Jagalchi Market for a seafood lunch, then walk to BIFF Square for a seed hotteok and look for the handprints. Cross Gwangbok-ro, then head north into Gukje Market and Kkangtong Market for fish cake tasting. Continue uphill to Bosu-dong Book Street for an hour of browsing in the afternoon light. If you still have energy, take a short taxi to Hwaguk Banjeom for an early dinner, or come back to Kkangtong for the night market at 7:30pm. Allow about five to six hours.

Jung-gu is crowded on weekends, and many small shops close on Sundays or on specific Mondays and Tuesdays, so check hours on Naver Map or KakaoMap before you go. If you stay longer, Busan Tower in Yongdusan Park and Gamcheon Culture Village are a short taxi ride away.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which films were shot at Bosu-dong Book Street?

Korea's public KCISA dataset lists The Attorney, where Song Kang-ho's character buys back his old exam books, and The King, where Jo In-sung runs through the alley. The drama Shopping King Louie filmed its episode 8 kiss scene here.

Where is the Crash Landing on You hotel in Busan?

The Commodore Hotel Busan at 중구로 151 in Jung-gu played the Pyongyang hotel visited by the Dooly couple. It is uphill from Jungang Station on Line 1, and easiest by short taxi.

How do I get to Nampo-dong from Busan Station?

Take Busan Metro Line 1 toward Sinpyeong for two to three stops to Nampo or Jagalchi Station. The ride takes under 10 minutes.

When does the Kkangtong night market open?

The dataset lists the Bupyeong Kkangtong night market as open daily from 7:30pm to 11:30pm, while the daytime market runs from 8am to 8pm on weekdays.

Is Jagalchi Market open every day?

The dataset lists Jagalchi Market as open daily from 5am to 10pm, closed on the 1st and 3rd Tuesday of each month. Individual stalls may keep their own hours.
