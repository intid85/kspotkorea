Why Seodaemun-gu Is a K-Drama Favorite

Seodaemun-gu sits just west of Seoul's historic downtown, linking Sinchon university streets, Yeonhui cafe hills and the Independence Gate park. Korea's public KCISA filming-location dataset lists about 224 records with a Seodaemun address — 150 from TV dramas, 44 from variety shows, 27 from artist visits and 3 from films — making it one of the densest drama districts north of the Han River.

This guide focuses on the places international fans actually plan a half-day around: Seodaemun Prison History Hall from While You Were Sleeping and many crime dramas, Yonsei University's Sinchon campus from Reply 1994 and Romance Is a Bonus Book, the Sinchon mural tunnel from Goblin, Ewha Womans University street, Swiss Grand Hotel in Yeonhui, and Sinchon nightlife spots like Panjajip from Our Beloved Summer. Every stop includes the exact Korean address so you can paste it into Naver Map or KakaoMap, or show it to a taxi driver.

Base yourself on Seoul Subway Line 2 (Sinchon and Ewha Womans University stations) and Line 3 (Dongnimmun). Walking between Sinchon, Yonsei and Ewha is easy; use a short taxi hop for the prison museum and Swiss Grand Hotel.

Seodaemun Prison History Hall and Independence Gate

Seodaemun Prison History Hall (서대문형무소역사관)

Seodaemun Prison History Hall (서울특별시 서대문구 통일로 251) is the district's most filmed heritage site, with 18 KCISA records. While You Were Sleeping uses the prison exterior when a character explains a wrongful-conviction suicide note. Money Flower, Vincenzo, Mad Dog, Doctor John, The World of the Married and Welcome, First Time in Korea? also list scenes here. The museum preserves the former colonial-era prison; closed Mondays. Check seasonal hours on Naver before you go (roughly 09:30–17:00 in winter, until 18:00 March–October).

Independence Gate / Dongnimmun Park (독립문)

Independence Gate sits in the same park cluster a few minutes from the prison museum. Arrive via Dongnimmun Station (Line 3) Exit 4–5 and walk five to eight minutes. After the museum, many visitors eat kimchi-jjim at Hanokjip main branch (한옥집 김치찜 본점, 서울특별시 서대문구 독립문로8길 17-1), a TV food-show favorite with six KCISA records from Amazing Saturday, Delicious Guys and Let's Eat 2.

Getting there: Show taxi drivers 서대문형무소역사관 or 통일로 251. Pair this stop with Yeonhui cafes afterward rather than walking back through traffic.

Yonsei Campus and the Sinchon Mural Tunnel

Yonsei University Sinchon Campus (연세대학교 신촌캠퍼스)

Yonsei University (서울특별시 서대문구 연세로 50) has 17 KCISA campus records. The film 1987 lists Kim Tae-ri's university scenes here. Romance Is a Bonus Book filmed campus walks; Cheer Up, True Beauty, Extraordinary You, Age of Youth, Iris, Abyss and Snowdrop also appear in the public dataset. Reply 1994 ties Sinchon Station and Yonsei Yeonhui Hall to the series' college vibe — the map pin for Underwood Hall's ivy courtyard is already on the K-Spot Korea interactive map.

Campus grounds are generally open to respectful visitors during daytime; do not enter classrooms or disrupt students. Enter from the Sinchon main gate toward Underwood Hall for the classic stone-and-ivy look.

Sinchon Mural Tunnel (신촌벽화터널)

The Sinchon mural tunnel (서울특별시 서대문구 대신동 143-28) sits on the approach between Sinchon and the Yonsei campus and has 11 KCISA records. In Goblin episode 10, Kim Shin (Gong Yoo) and the Grim Reaper (Lee Dong-wook) walk the tunnel like a runway after buying green onions for Ji Eun-tak. While You Were Sleeping also lists a police chase scene here, along with Record of Youth, Touch Your Heart, First Love Again and Oh! Master. The murals change over time — go for the tunnel atmosphere and the Goblin photo angle rather than expecting identical graffiti.

Sinchon Streets, Panjajip and Ewha

Sinchon nightlife: Panjajip and Myeongmul-gil (판자집 · 명물길)

Panjajip (판자집, 서울특별시 서대문구 연세로2길 29) is a Sinchon bar-restaurant with nine KCISA drama records. Our Beloved Summer episode 12 sends idol NJ and PD Kim Ji-woong here to drink away first-love pain. Romance Is a Bonus Book, Good Doctor, Itaewon Class, Undercover and Kiss Sixth Sense also list scenes. Hours skew late — roughly 17:00 until early morning — so treat it as an evening stop.

Nearby Myeongmul-gil (명물길) appears in A-Teen, Chicago Typewriter, Rich Man and My Fellow Citizens!. Sinchon Station (신촌역, 서울특별시 서대문구 신촌역로 30) itself shows up in Reply 1994 and Beautiful Days. Use Line 2 Exit 2–3 for Yonsei-ro and the campus side.

Ewha Womans University street (이화여대길)

Ewha Womans University street (서울특별시 서대문구 이화여대길) runs from Line 2 Ewha Womans University Station toward campus. The street itself is listed for My Holo Love (계룡선녀전); the campus address (이화여대길 52) appears in the film The King and the drama Love Alarm. Note that Ewha's main campus straddles the Seodaemun–Mapo edge — this guide focuses on the Seodaemun-side shopping street and approaches. Pair it with Sinchon in the same Line 2 hop (one stop apart).

Swiss Grand Hotel, Yeonhui and Local Food

Swiss Grand Hotel, Yeonhui (스위스 그랜드 호텔)

Swiss Grand Hotel (서울특별시 서대문구 연희로 353) leads Seodaemun's KCISA list with 20 hotel records. Dramas use it as a stand-in luxury hotel: Good Casting (cafe reunion), Navillera (70th birthday stay), Good Job (as "Royal Hotel"), Business Proposal, A Gentleman and a Young Lady, Suspicious Partner, Melancholia, Record of Youth and Bride of the Water God 2017. Lobby and cafe areas are the usual visitor-friendly zones — confirm dress code and photography rules at the desk.

Yeonhui-dong around the hotel is a quiet residential cafe neighborhood. Yeonhui-dong Kalguksu main branch (연희동칼국수 본점) appears on food shows and artist recommendation lists. Combine a hotel exterior photo with a Yeonhui coffee stop after Sinchon.

Where to eat in Seodaemun

Besides Hanokjip kimchi-jjim near Independence Gate and Panjajip for late drinks, Sinchon street food along Yonsei-ro covers hotteok, tteokbokki and student-priced meals. For a sit-down lunch after the museum, aim for the Dongnimmun area; for dinner after campus photos, stay in Sinchon or ride one stop to Ewha. Hours change — always check Naver Map before you go, and show the Korean name to staff if English menus are limited.

Half-Day Seodaemun K-Spot Itinerary

Start at Dongnimmun Station (Line 3) for Seodaemun Prison History Hall and Independence Gate in the morning (90–120 minutes including museum ticket time). Taxi or bus toward Sinchon for lunch, then walk Yonsei campus and the mural tunnel in the early afternoon. Continue along Yonsei-ro to Myeongmul-gil and Panjajip if you want nightlife photos later, or ride Line 2 one stop to Ewha Womans University street for shopping and campus exteriors.

If Swiss Grand Hotel is on your list, add a late-afternoon taxi to Yeonhui-ro 353 and cafe-hop in Yeonhui-dong before returning via Hongdae or Sinchon. The whole loop fits a half-day without a car if you use Line 2 + Line 3 and one or two short taxis.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get to Seodaemun Prison History Hall by subway?

Take Seoul Subway Line 3 to Dongnimmun Station and use Exit 4 or 5. Walk about five to eight minutes to 서울특별시 서대문구 통일로 251. The museum is closed on Mondays.

Where is the Goblin mural tunnel in Sinchon?

Sinchon Mural Tunnel (신촌벽화터널) is listed at 서울특별시 서대문구 대신동 143-28 on the approach between Sinchon and Yonsei University. In Goblin episode 10, Gong Yoo and Lee Dong-wook walk through it. Murals change, so expect the tunnel shape more than identical graffiti.

Can visitors enter Yonsei University campus?

Daytime outdoor areas of Yonsei University Sinchon Campus (서울특별시 서대문구 연세로 50) are generally open to respectful visitors. Do not enter classrooms or disrupt students. Underwood Hall and the main gate area are the usual drama photo spots.

Which dramas filmed at Swiss Grand Hotel in Yeonhui?

The KCISA dataset lists about 20 records for Swiss Grand Hotel (서울특별시 서대문구 연희로 353), including Good Casting, Navillera, Good Job, Business Proposal, Suspicious Partner and Record of Youth. It is often used as a stand-in luxury hotel.

Is Ewha Womans University in Seodaemun-gu or Mapo-gu?

The campus straddles the district edge. This guide focuses on Ewha street on the Seodaemun side (서울특별시 서대문구 이화여대길), reached from Line 2 Ewha Womans University Station. It pairs easily with Sinchon one stop away.

Where did Our Beloved Summer and Romance Is a Bonus Book film in Sinchon?

Both list Panjajip (판자집, 서울특별시 서대문구 연세로2길 29). Romance Is a Bonus Book also lists Yonsei campus and nearby Sinchon restaurants. Go in the evening — the venue opens late afternoon.
