Naksan Park in Seoul: K-Drama Filming Location Guide (My Mister, The King 2 Hearts)

Naksan Park (낙산공원), the hilltop park on the Seoul City Wall above Daehangno, has 29 records in Korea's public KCISA filming dataset, plus four more on the wall-side West Road. This guide shows which scenes of My Mister, The King 2 Hearts, Because This Is My First Life, Run On and more were filmed on its ridge, and how to walk up from Hyehwa or Dongdaemun.

Why Naksan Park Is One of Seoul's Most Filmed Viewpoints

Naksan Park (낙산공원) sits on Naksan, a low hill on the eastern edge of old Seoul in Jongno-gu, right above the theater district of Daehangno. The name means "camel mountain", because the hill was said to look like a camel's hump. Its ridge carries one of the best-preserved stretches of the Seoul City Wall (한양도성), the stone fortress wall that surrounded the Joseon capital, and from the top you look across rooftops to Bukhansan, Namsan Tower and the towers of Dongdaemun.

That combination of an old stone wall, a free park open around the clock, and a wide night view is exactly what Korean location managers want for a quiet conversation, a confession or a late-night walk. Korea's public KCISA filming-location dataset (한국문화정보원 미디어 촬영지 데이터) lists 29 records at the Naksan Park address (낙산길 41): 25 TV dramas, 2 films, 1 variety show and 1 artist entry. Four more records sit on the Naksan Fortress West Road (낙산성곽서길), the wall-side path that leads up to the park from Ihwa-dong.

This guide covers only Naksan Park: which scenes the public data places here, how to walk up from Hyehwa or Dongdaemun, the exact Korean address for a taxi, food and cafes nearby, and when the view is at its best.

What Was Filmed at Naksan Park (Public Data)

Everything below comes from the KCISA records for 낙산공원 (서울특별시 종로구 낙산길 41) and 낙산성곽서길. Where a record is vague, we kept only what it says.

Late-night talks and confessions

My Mister (나의 아저씨) is the record many fans look for: in episode 5, this is where the grandmother is wheeled up in a cart so she can see the moon, one of the drama's gentlest scenes. In The King 2 Hearts (더킹 투하츠), episode 7, Princess Lee Jae-shin (Lee Yoon-ji) and royal guard Eun Si-kyung (Jo Jung-suk) sit on the fortress wall, make wishes on a shooting star, and he falls for her as she sings about first love.

Because This Is My First Life (이번 생은 처음이라) has three entries. In episode 7, Yeon Bok-nam (Kim Min-kyu) brings Yoon Ji-ho (Jung So-min) here by motorbike to show her the view, and they return in episode 8. In episode 9, Nam Se-hee (Lee Min-ki) runs up the Naksan Fortress West Road toward the park. Radio Romance (라디오 로맨스) episode 9 has Ji Soo-ho (Yoon Doo-joon) and Song Geu-rim (Kim So-hyun) talking about their families here before she finally hugs him, and in Let Me Hear Your Song (너의 노래를 들려줘) episode 4, Jang Yoon (Yeon Woo-jin) brings Hong I-young (Kim Se-jeong) to the park and asks if he can kiss her.

In the final episode 16 of Dae Jang Geum Is Watching (대장금이 보고 있다), Han San-hae (Shin Dong-wook) proposes to Bok Seung-ah (Kwon Yu-ri) here. Twelve Nights (열두밤) uses the park twice for Cha Hyun-oh (Shin Hyun-soo) and Han Yu-kyung (Han Seung-yeon) during their 2010 stay in Seoul, including the bench scene where she falls asleep on his shoulder. Run On (런온) episode 11 shows Ki Sun-gyeom (Im Si-wan) and Oh Mi-joo (Shin Se-kyung) walking up the park on an evening date, and in 18 Again (18 어게인) episode 1, young Hong Dae-young (Lee Do-hyun) and Jung Da-jung talk about what it means to become young parents.

Morning runs and everyday Seoul

The hill is also a favorite for daily-life scenes. Misaeng (미생) opens episode 8 with Jang Geu-rae (Im Si-wan) on a morning jog here, and in Today's Webtoon (오늘의 웹툰) On Ma-eum (Kim Se-jeong) trains by jogging in the park every morning. Strong Girl Bong-soon (힘쎈여자 도봉순) episode 7 shows Do Bong-soon (Park Bo-young) spending time with her mother and grandmother at the park playground. Should We Kiss First? (키스 먼저 할까요) episode 1 has Son Moo-han (Kam Woo-sung) walking his dog here to think creatively, and Rooftop Prince (옥탑방 왕세자) episode 12 has the characters resting on the fortress wall.

Other drama records include 365: Repeat the Year (Shin Ga-hyun, played by Nam Ji-hyun, comes to draw at night in episode 2), Can You Hear My Heart, Come Back Mister, Liar Game, Undercover, One Dollar Lawyer, Over the Rainbow, The Secret Life of My Secretary, Feast of the Gods, Pots of Gold and the weekend drama Beautiful Love, Wonderful Life. On the West Road, the data adds Brilliant Legacy (찬란한 유산), Touch (터치, 2020) and The Second Husband (두 번째 남편).

Films, a dating show and an artist

Two films are listed: Assassination (암살), with the record describing Naksan as one of Seoul's signature night-view spots used in the film, and Confidential Assignment (공조), where Yoo Hae-jin's character is caught in a lie. The dating show Heart Signal (하트시그널) filmed a walk here, and the dataset also tags singer-actor Rain (비) with a meeting scene at the park from one of his dramas.

Walking Naksan Park and the City Wall

Naksan Park is small, and most visitors spend 45 to 90 minutes walking the ridge. Paths are paved but steep in places, so wear comfortable shoes.

The hilltop plaza and observation area

The top of the hill opens into a plaza with benches and a viewing area facing north and west. This is where most of the bench and conversation scenes feel like they were shot: the wall drops away below you and the mountains line the horizon.

The fortress wall path

A path runs along the inside of the Seoul City Wall for the whole length of the park. Walk it south and downhill and it continues to Heunginjimun (흥인지문, Dongdaemun Gate); walk it north and it leads toward Hyehwamun and Seongbuk-dong. You can also step through small gates to the outer side of the wall, where the stones look older and the view is less crowded. Please do not climb or sit on the wall in places where signs prohibit it, even if a drama character did.

Ihwa-dong and the West Road

The western slope below the park is Ihwa-dong (이화동), a hillside neighborhood known for its stairs and murals. The Naksan Fortress West Road (낙산성곽서길) climbs between the village and the wall, and it is the most scenic way up. Ihwa-dong is a residential area: keep your voice down, do not photograph people's homes or doors up close, and stay on public paths.

Address, Hours and How to Get There

Exact address for a taxi

Show the driver 낙산공원 or the address 서울특별시 종로구 낙산길 41. It works in Naver Map, KakaoMap and Kakao T. A taxi from Myeongdong or Insadong usually takes about 15 to 20 minutes, depending on traffic.

Opening hours and fees

According to the public data, Naksan Park is open 24 hours a day, every day of the year (매일 00시 - 24시, 연중무휴). It is a public park, so there is no admission fee. The phone number listed is 02-743-7985.

By subway from Hyehwa Station (Line 4)

Take Seoul Subway Line 4 to Hyehwa Station (혜화역) and leave by Exit 2. Walk through Marronnier Park and up through Ihwa-dong; it is about 15 minutes uphill on foot. Type 낙산공원 into Naver Map or KakaoMap for walking directions, since Google Maps does not give full walking routes in Korea.

By village bus

If you would rather skip the climb, the green village bus Jongno 03 (종로03) runs from near Hyehwa Station up to a stop at the park entrance (낙산공원 정류장). Check the current route and stop in Naver Map before you go, because village bus routes are occasionally adjusted. Seoul village buses accept T-money and most contactless transit cards.

From Dongdaemun Station (Lines 1 and 4)

For a longer walk, start at Dongdaemun Station (동대문역), go to Heunginjimun Gate, and follow the city wall trail uphill. It takes roughly 20 to 30 minutes to reach the hilltop and is one of the easiest sections of the Seoul City Wall to walk.

Where to Eat and Drink Near Naksan Park

The KCISA dataset also lists TV-featured cafes and restaurants within a short walk. Hours below are from the public data and may have changed, so check Naver Map before you go.

Hakrim Coffee (학림, 종로구 대학로 119 2층) is a long-running Daehangno coffee house on the second floor across from Marronnier Park. The data lists it in My Love from the Star, The Heirs, Youth of May and Because This Is My First Life, listed as open daily 10am to 11pm. In Ihwa-dong, right below the park, Cafe Gaebbul (카페 개뿔, 종로구 낙산성곽서1길 26) appeared on I Live Alone, and Chaek Ingneun Goyangi (책읽는고양이, 낙산성곽서1길 18-2), a small coffee spot, was featured on 2TV Saengsaeng Information.

For a meal in Daehangno, Sundae Sillok (순대실록, 종로구 동숭길 127) is the Korean blood-sausage restaurant visited in Let's Eat 2, and Dodoya (도도야, 동숭길 39) is a Japanese kamameshi (pot rice) restaurant that the data notes as a regular haunt of director Park Chan-wook. On the east side of the hill, the cafe Tertre (테르트르, 종로구 낙산5길 46) was featured on You Quiz on the Block for its view over Changsin-dong.

Visit Tips: Best Time, Photos and Etiquette

Go at sunset and stay after dark. The wall faces west toward Bukhansan and Inwangsan, so the hour before sunset is the classic time. Once the lights come on along the wall path, you get the night view that the Assassination record describes and that so many drama confessions use.

Mornings for quiet. If you want the Misaeng and Today's Webtoon feeling, come early. You will share the path with local joggers and walkers rather than photo groups.

Seasons. Spring brings cherry and forsythia blossoms along the wall, and autumn leaves color the slopes in late October to early November. Winter evenings are cold and windy on the ridge; bring a layer even in spring and fall.

Combine it. Naksan Park pairs well with an afternoon show in Daehangno, or with Dongdaemun Design Plaza and the Dongdaemun markets after you walk down the wall to Heunginjimun.

Respect the heritage. The Seoul City Wall is a national historic site. Follow signs about where you may stand, keep tripods off narrow paths, take your trash with you, and keep noise low near the homes of Ihwa-dong, especially at night.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which K-dramas were filmed at Naksan Park?

The KCISA public dataset lists 25 dramas at Naksan Park, including My Mister, The King 2 Hearts, Because This Is My First Life, Radio Romance, Let Me Hear Your Song, Run On, Misaeng, Strong Girl Bong-soon, Rooftop Prince, 18 Again, Twelve Nights and Should We Kiss First?, plus the films Assassination and Confidential Assignment and the show Heart Signal.

What is the address of Naksan Park for a taxi?

Show 낙산공원 or 서울특별시 종로구 낙산길 41. It works in Naver Map, KakaoMap and Kakao T.

Is Naksan Park open at night, and is it free?

Yes. The public data lists it as open 24 hours, every day of the year. It is a public park with no admission fee.

How do I get to Naksan Park by subway?

Take Line 4 to Hyehwa Station, Exit 2, and walk about 15 minutes uphill through Ihwa-dong, or take the Jongno 03 village bus to the park. You can also walk up the city wall trail from Dongdaemun Station and Heunginjimun in about 20 to 30 minutes.

Where was the My Mister moon scene filmed?

According to the KCISA dataset, the episode 5 scene of My Mister where the grandmother is brought up in a cart to see the moon was filmed at Naksan Park in Jongno-gu, Seoul.
