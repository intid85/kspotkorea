Gwanghallu Garden in Namwon: K-Drama Filming Location Guide (Love in the Moonlight, The Red Sleeve)

Gwanghallu Garden (광한루원), the Joseon-era garden of the Chunhyang love story in Namwon, has 17 records in Korea's public KCISA filming dataset, more than almost any palace-style location outside Seoul. This guide shows which scenes of Love in the Moonlight, The Red Sleeve, Moon Lovers and more were filmed at its pavilion, Magpie Bridge and pond, and how to visit from Seoul in a day.

Why Gwanghallu Garden Is Korea's Favorite Joseon Palace Stand-In

Gwanghallu Garden (광한루원) in Namwon, North Jeolla, is one of the most famous traditional gardens in Korea. Its centerpiece, Gwanghallu Pavilion, is a red-pillared Joseon-era pavilion that is designated a national Treasure. Around it, a large pond holds small islands, a four-arched stone bridge and a second pavilion on stilts. The garden is also the setting of Chunhyangjeon (The Tale of Chunhyang), Korea's best-known love story, in which Chunhyang and Lee Mong-ryong first meet here on the day of the Dano festival.

That mix of water, old wood and stone makes the garden look like a royal palace on screen, even though it is a provincial site several hours south of Seoul. Korea's public KCISA filming-location dataset (한국문화정보원 미디어 촬영지 데이터) lists 17 separate records at this one address: 15 TV dramas, one film and one K-pop variety show. Several of the biggest historical dramas of the last 15 years used the garden as part of the palace, which is why many visitors recognize it before they know its name.

This guide covers only Gwanghallu Garden: what was filmed where according to the public data, how to get there from Seoul, the exact Korean address for a taxi, where to eat nearby, and how to time your visit.

What Was Filmed at Gwanghallu Garden (Public Data)

Everything in this section comes from the KCISA records for 광한루원 (요천로 1447). Where a record is unclear or looks garbled, we left out the detail rather than guess.

Historical dramas: the garden as a royal palace

Love in the Moonlight (구르미 그린 달빛) is the record most fans ask about. In episode 1, the crown prince played by Park Bo-gum pretends to study in Yeongjugak, the small pavilion on the garden's Bongnae island, while his father the king walks past. In episode 5, Princess Myeongeun (Jung Hye-sung) invites Hong Ra-on (Kim Yoo-jung) for a boat ride on the pond, and Ra-on ends up falling into the water while the crown prince watches.

The Red Sleeve (옷소매 붉은 끝동) uses the garden as part of the palace; in episode 3, Crown Prince Yi San (Lee Jun-ho) and Deok-im (Lee Se-young) meet in a scene set up by Princess Cheongyeon. In Rookie Historian Goo Hae-ryung (신입사관 구해령), episode 1 shows Prince Yi Rim (Cha Eun-woo) crossing the bridge. In The King's Affection (연모), episode 4 has Crown Prince Lee Hwi (Park Eun-bin) crossing a bridge with attendants while new guard Kim Ga-on (Choi Byung-chan) breaks the rule of walking five steps behind.

Moon Lovers: Scarlet Heart Ryeo (달의 연인 - 보보경심 려) filmed an episode 4 scene on a bridge here after Hae-soo (IU) is rescued, an almost-kiss that gets interrupted. 100 Days My Prince (백일의 낭군님) used Gwanghallu, Ojakgyo and Wanwoljeong as the palace garden where the main characters meet and talk. Queen: Love and War (간택 - 여인들의 전쟁) sends the queen candidates across Ojakgyo in episode 1, and in episode 11 the king and Kang Eun-bo (Jin Se-yeon) share a kiss on Bongnae island.

The dataset also lists Jang Ok-jung, Living by Love (장옥정, 사랑에 살다), with royal bride-selection scenes at Wanwoljeong in episodes 3 and 4; Rooftop Prince (옥탑방 왕세자), whose Joseon flashbacks place the crown princess's drowning at the garden's pond; Lovers of the Red Sky (홍천기), where a ritual in episode 1 takes place here; The Slave Hunters (추노); and the classic anthology Hometown Legends (전설의 고향), which uses Ojakgyo in its final scene.

Modern dramas, film and K-pop

Not every record is a costume drama. In My Roommate Is a Gumiho (간 떨어지는 동거), episode 1, Lee Dam (Hyeri) and her friend visit for a school project, put on hanbok and sit on the bridge in front of Gwanghallu acting out a historical drama scene. In Record of Youth (청춘기록), episode 9, Sa Hye-jun (Park Bo-gum) is shown filming a drama here. Delightful Girl Choon-hyang (쾌걸 춘향), a modern retelling of the Chunhyang story, stages the first meeting of Chun-hyang (Han Chae-young) and Mong-ryong (Jae Hee) here in episode 1.

The only film in the records is Masquerade (광해, 2012), with the scene where Lee Byung-hun reads a poem to Han Hyo-joo. For K-pop fans, VICTON filmed the opening of episode 11 (North Jeolla) of the travel show Tour Avatar here.

Walking the Garden: Gwanghallu, Ojakgyo, Wanwoljeong and Wolmae's House

The garden is flat and compact, and a relaxed loop takes about an hour, or 90 minutes with photos. After the ticket gate, follow the path to the pond and you will see the main sights in this order.

Gwanghallu Pavilion (광한루)

The pavilion stands on tall stone pillars beside the pond. Visitors are generally not allowed up on the floor, so the classic photo is from the pond edge in front of it, framing the roof and its reflection. This is the angle you will recognize from many palace scenes.

Ojakgyo, the Magpie Bridge (오작교)

The long stone bridge with four arches is named after the bridge of magpies in the Korean legend of Gyeonu and Jiknyeo, two lovers who can meet only once a year. Walking across it is said to bring luck in love, which is why couples in dramas keep ending up on it. Large koi gather under the bridge; you can usually buy fish food near the pond.

Wanwoljeong and the islands (완월정)

Wanwoljeong, the colorful pavilion standing in the water, is the moon-viewing pavilion where the dataset places the bride-selection scenes. The small islands in the pond represent the mythical mountains where immortals were believed to live; Bongnae island with Yeongjugak is where Park Bo-gum's crown prince "studies" in Love in the Moonlight.

Chunhyang shrine and Wolmae's House

Toward the back of the garden is a shrine with a portrait of Chunhyang, and Wolmae's House (월매집), a group of thatched-roof buildings recreating the home of Chunhyang's mother. It is the quietest corner and a good place for traditional hanok photos without the crowds at the pond.

Address, Hours and How to Get There

Exact address for a taxi

Show the driver 광한루원 or the address 전북특별자치도 남원시 요천로 1447. The KCISA data lists it under the older province name 전라북도 남원시 요천로 1447; both work in Naver Map, KakaoMap and Kakao T.

Opening hours

According to the public data, the garden is open every day of the year, 8am to 7pm in summer and 8am to 6pm in winter. After the paid hours it stays open free of charge for two more hours (until 9pm in summer and 8pm in winter), when the pavilions are lit. There is an admission fee during the day; confirm the current price on the Namwon City tourism site or at the gate. Phone: 063-625-4861.

From Seoul by train

Namwon Station (남원역) is on the KTX Jeolla Line toward Yeosu. From Yongsan Station the ride usually takes around two to two and a half hours depending on the train. From Namwon Station, a taxi to Gwanghallu Garden takes roughly 10 minutes; local city buses also run toward downtown, but a taxi is simpler on a day trip.

By intercity or express bus

Buses run from Seoul and other cities to the Namwon bus terminals in the center of town. From there the garden is close enough to walk in about 15 to 20 minutes, or it is a very short taxi ride. Check the terminal name on your ticket and type 광한루원 into Naver Map or KakaoMap for walking directions; Google Maps does not give full walking directions in Korea.

Where to Eat Near Gwanghallu Garden

Namwon is best known in Korea for chueotang (추어탕), a thick, peppery loach soup, and many restaurants around the garden specialize in it. If you want something milder, order it with rice and add perilla powder and chopped chili to taste.

The KCISA dataset also lists several TV-featured restaurants in Namwon. Gyeongbangnu (경방루, 남원시 광한북로 29), just north of the garden, appeared on Baek Jong-won's Top 3 Chef King (백종원의 3대천왕) for its long-running sweet and sour pork. Baktorang (박토랑, 남원시 광한서로 50), on the west side, was featured on Korean Table (한국인의 밥상) for gourd and perilla dishes. In the town center, the bakery Myeongmun Jegwa (명문제과, 남원시 용성로 56) was on Top 3 Chef King for its cream soboro bread, an easy snack for the train home. Hours change, so check Naver Map before you go.

Visit Tips: Best Time, Photos and Etiquette

Go early or go late. Mornings right after 8am are calm and the light on the pavilion is soft. On the other end, staying for the free evening hours lets you see Gwanghallu and Ojakgyo lit up and reflected in the pond, as in the photo above.

Seasons. Spring brings blossoms and the Chunhyang Festival (춘향제), Namwon's biggest event, usually held around May, when the garden is busiest. Autumn color and still winter mornings are quieter. In summer, bring water and a hat; there is shade under the old trees but the paths get hot.

Hanbok photos. Like the characters in My Roommate Is a Gumiho, many visitors wear hanbok here. Rental shops operate near the garden; prices and hours vary, so search 남원 한복대여 on Naver Map. Do not climb on stone walls or lean over the pond for a shot.

Respect the heritage. Gwanghallu is a national Treasure. Follow the signs on where you may walk, keep tripods out of the main paths when it is crowded, and do not feed the fish anything other than the food sold on site.

Make it a day trip. If you have extra time, the KCISA data lists other Namwon filming spots outside the garden, such as Namwon Land (남원랜드) in Youth of May and the old Seodo Station film site (구서도역 영상촬영장) in Mr. Sunshine. They need a taxi, so treat them as separate stops.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which K-dramas were filmed at Gwanghallu Garden?

The KCISA public dataset lists 15 dramas at Gwanghallu Garden, including Love in the Moonlight, The Red Sleeve, Moon Lovers: Scarlet Heart Ryeo, 100 Days My Prince, Rookie Historian Goo Hae-ryung, The King's Affection, Rooftop Prince, Jang Ok-jung, Lovers of the Red Sky, Queen: Love and War, My Roommate Is a Gumiho and Record of Youth, plus the film Masquerade and VICTON's Tour Avatar.

What is the address of Gwanghallu Garden for a taxi?

Show 광한루원 or 전북특별자치도 남원시 요천로 1447 (listed in the public data as 전라북도 남원시 요천로 1447). It works in Naver Map, KakaoMap and Kakao T.

What are the opening hours of Gwanghallu Garden?

The public data lists 8am to 7pm in summer and 8am to 6pm in winter, open every day, with free entry for two hours after closing time (until 9pm in summer and 8pm in winter). Check the official Namwon site for the current admission fee.

How do I get to Gwanghallu Garden from Seoul?

Take the KTX on the Jeolla Line from Yongsan to Namwon Station (around 2 to 2.5 hours), then a taxi of about 10 minutes. Intercity and express buses also run to Namwon's bus terminals, about a 15 to 20 minute walk from the garden.

Where did Park Bo-gum film Love in the Moonlight in Namwon?

According to the KCISA data, in episode 1 his crown prince pretends to study in Yeongjugak on the Bongnae island of Gwanghallu Garden's pond, and in episode 5 Hong Ra-on falls into the pond during a boat ride with Princess Myeongeun.
