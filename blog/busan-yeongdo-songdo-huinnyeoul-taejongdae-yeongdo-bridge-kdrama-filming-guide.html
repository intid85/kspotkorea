Busan Yeongdo & Songdo (Huinnyeoul Village, Taejongdae, Yeongdo Bridge): K-Drama & Movie Filming Spots Guide

Korea's public KCISA filming-location dataset lists 36 records in Busan's Yeongdo-gu and Seo-gu, from Huinnyeoul Culture Village in The Attorney and the National Maritime Museum in Life on Mars to Chungmu-dong Dawn Market in The King: Eternal Monarch. This guide links Yeongdo Bridge, Huinnyeoul, Taejongdae and the Songdo coast into one half day from Nampo Station.

Why Yeongdo and Songdo Belong on Your Busan Screen Trip

Most visitors see Busan from Haeundae or Gwangalli, but the island district of Yeongdo-gu and the Songdo coast in neighboring Seo-gu show a different side of the city: steep hillside lanes, a working harbor, sea cliffs and Korea's first public beach. Both sit just across the water from the old downtown of Nampo-dong, so they pair naturally with a Jung-gu day.

Korea's public KCISA filming-location dataset (한국문화정보원 미디어 촬영지 데이터) lists 24 records with a Yeongdo-gu address and 12 in Seo-gu. They mix a few strong screen locations with a long list of TV food picks. The film The Attorney recreated the 1980s at Huinnyeoul Culture Village, the drama Life on Mars turned the National Maritime Museum into a Seoul police headquarters, The King: Eternal Monarch filmed at Chungmu-dong Dawn Market, and Shopping King Louie used a terrace on Cheonmasan. Kang Daniel, who grew up in Busan, recommended Huinnyeoul, Taejongdae and Yeongdo Bridge for a Yeongdo tour, and Infinite Challenge, THE BOYZ, NCT, SF9 and 2 Days & 1 Night all appear too.

Huinnyeoul Culture Village: The Attorney and Infinite Challenge

Huinnyeoul Culture Village (흰여울문화마을) is a row of small houses painted white and pastel, stacked along a cliff above the outer anchorage of Busan port. The name means a stream that runs white like snow, after the water that once ran down the slope into the sea. In the dataset it is the location where The Attorney recreated 1980s Busan, where Infinite Challenge episode 459 sent the cast to find disguise clothes, and where THE BOYZ members Sangyeon, Younghoon and Eric carried out a mission.

The main lane, Huinnyeoul-gil, is lined with small cafes and viewpoints over the ships waiting offshore. Below it, the Jeoryeong coastal path (절영해안산책로) runs along the water and ends at a short tunnel that is a favorite photo spot. Remember that this is a residential village: people live behind those doors, so keep your voice down and avoid photographing into homes.

Getting there

There is no subway on Yeongdo. From Nampo Station (Line 1), cross to the bus stops on the Yeongdo Bridge side and take a city bus to the Huinnyeoul Culture Village (흰여울문화마을) stop on Jeoryeong-ro; KakaoMap shows the current routes. A taxi from Nampo takes about 10 minutes. For the driver, show 흰여울문화마을 or 부산광역시 영도구 영선동4가 605-3.

Yeongdo Bridge and the Yeongdo Food Picks

Yeongdo Bridge (영도대교) links Nampo-dong to the island. It is a drawbridge, and according to the dataset its span is raised once a day from 2:00pm to 2:15pm. That daily lift is one of the stops Kang Daniel picked for his Yeongdo tour. Watch from the Nampo-dong side or from the Yeongdo end at Daegyo-dong, and arrive a little early because the crowd builds before two.

Where to eat and drink on Yeongdo

Yeongdo has a strong local food list in the dataset. Dalttne (달뜨네, 절영로 205) serves mackerel sashimi and sirak gukbap and appears on Battle Trip and Korean Table; Kang Daniel also ate here with a teacher from his old school. It opens Wednesday to Monday from 5pm to 10pm. Jaegi Dwaeji Gukbap (재기돼지국밥, 절영로49번길 25) is a pork soup house from Baek Jong-won's Top 3 Chef King, open daily from 8am. Kang Daniel's favorite tteokbokki is at Donald Tteokbokki (도날드 떡볶이, 남항새싹길 9), open Monday to Saturday. For something sweet, Imodonas (이모도나스, 절영로 134) sells donuts and croquettes from Live Today Morning.

Drama fans should look for Yang Dabang (양다방, 대평로 49), a retro coffee house where Jung Kyung-ho and Go Ah-sung investigate the "Yang Dabang murder" in Life on Mars. The dataset lists it as open Monday to Saturday from 7:30am to 6pm and Sunday from 9am.

Taejongdae and the National Maritime Museum

Taejongdae (태종대, 전망로 24) is the park at the southern tip of Yeongdo, with pine forest, sea cliffs and views toward Japan's Tsushima on clear days. It is another of Kang Daniel's picks. The loop road inside the park is closed to private cars, so walk it or ride the Danubi tour train, which stops at the observatory, the lighthouse and Taejong Temple. The dataset lists the park as open from 4am in summer and 5am in winter until midnight.

Near the park entrance, Taejongdae Sarangbang (태종대 사랑방 조개구이, 태종로 816 2층) is the grilled clam restaurant where NCT's Jaemin and Jeno ate on Battle Trip, also featured on Food Road. On the coast nearby, Chungbukjip Nunaya (충북집 누나야, 감지해변길 90) at Gamji Beach is the seafood tent Lee Seung-gi visited with baseball star Lee Dae-ho on 2 Days & 1 Night.

National Maritime Museum

The National Maritime Museum (국립해양박물관, 해양로301번길 45) sits on the harbor side of Yeongdo. In Life on Mars it stood in for the Seoul Metropolitan Police headquarters. Inside are ship models, an aquarium tunnel and a children's area. The dataset lists it as open Tuesday to Friday from 9am to 6pm and to 7pm on weekends, closed Mondays.

Getting there

City buses run from Nampo Station to the Taejongdae bus stop in about 25 to 30 minutes; check KakaoMap for the current route. The museum is a short taxi ride from Taejongdae. For a taxi, show 태종대 or 부산광역시 영도구 전망로 24.

Songdo, Seo-gu: Cable Car, Skywalk and Dawn Market

Across the harbor mouth, Songdo Beach in Seo-gu opened in 1913 as Korea's first public beach. Today its draws are the Songdo Marine Cable Car, which carries cabins over the bay, and the Songdo Cloud Trail (송도구름산책로), a curved skywalk with glass panels over the water. In the dataset, SF9's Chani, Dawon and Inseong rode the cable car on Battle Trip, and it is listed as open daily from 9am to 9pm. NCT filmed a segment at Cafe Haein (카페 해인, 암남로94번길 62), a sea-view cafe open daily from 11:30am to 11pm.

For seafood, Samyeong Hoetjip (삼영횟집, 송도해변로 83-1) is the cold raw-fish soup place from 6 O'Clock My Hometown. Inland, Yeongnam Naengmyeon Milmyeon (영남냉면밀면, 보수대로201번길 7) serves Busan's signature cold wheat noodles from Battle Trip episode 13.

Two more drama spots

Chungmu-dong Dawn Market (충무동 새벽시장, 해안새벽시장길 68) is the harbor market where Prime Minister Koo Seo-ryung, played by Jung Eun-chae, arrives with her entourage in episode 3 of The King: Eternal Monarch. Come early in the morning to see it at its busiest. Up on Cheonmasan, Cheonmasan Eco House (천마산에코하우스, 천마산로 342) is the guesthouse terrace where Oh Dae-hwan's character invites Lim Se-mi's in Shopping King Louie episode 12; it is also a stay, so treat it as a viewpoint unless you book a room.

Getting there

A taxi from Jagalchi or Nampo Station to Songdo Beach takes about 10 minutes. Several city buses also run there from Nampo. Show 송도해수욕장 or 송도해상케이블카 to the driver.

A Half-Day Yeongdo and Songdo Itinerary

Start at Nampo Station around 1:30pm and walk to Yeongdo Bridge for the 2pm lift. Cross to Yeongdo, then take a short taxi or bus to Huinnyeoul Culture Village and spend an hour on the lane and the coastal path. Continue to Taejongdae by bus or taxi and ride the Danubi train for sunset views. Finish with grilled clams near the park entrance, or with mackerel sashimi at Dalttne when it opens at 5pm. Allow about five to six hours.

If you prefer mornings, reverse the plan: begin at Chungmu-dong Dawn Market, ride the Songdo cable car, walk the skywalk, then cross to Yeongdo for lunch and Huinnyeoul. Hills on Yeongdo are steep, so wear good shoes, and check opening hours on Naver Map or KakaoMap before you go, because the dataset times may have changed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where was The Attorney filmed in Busan?

Korea's public KCISA dataset lists Huinnyeoul Culture Village on Yeongdo as the place where the film recreated 1980s Busan. The movie also filmed at Bosu-dong Book Street in nearby Jung-gu.

What time does Yeongdo Bridge open?

The dataset lists the drawbridge lift as daily from 2:00pm to 2:15pm. Arrive a little early to get a good viewing spot on the Nampo-dong side.

Is there a subway to Huinnyeoul Culture Village or Taejongdae?

No. Take Busan Metro Line 1 to Nampo Station, then a city bus or a short taxi. Huinnyeoul is about 10 minutes by taxi and Taejongdae about 20 minutes.

Which drama used the National Maritime Museum?

In the drama Life on Mars, the National Maritime Museum in Yeongdo appeared as the Seoul Metropolitan Police headquarters. The museum is closed on Mondays.

Can I combine Yeongdo with Nampo-dong and Jagalchi?

Yes. Yeongdo Bridge starts right beside Nampo-dong, so you can spend the morning in Jung-gu's markets and the afternoon on Yeongdo or in Songdo.
