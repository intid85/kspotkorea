Section 01

About K-Spot Korea & Editorial Policy

K-Spot Korea (kspotkorea.co.kr) is an independent English-language cultural travel guide and interactive mapping platform dedicated to international travelers exploring South Korea through K-Drama, K-Pop, and Korean Cinema filming locations.

Public Data & On-the-Ground Verification: Our database and daily regional guides are built upon verified open cultural datasets published by the Korea Cultural Information Service Agency (한국문화정보원 / KCISA) and regional tourism boards, combined with curated English travel context, exact Korean road-name addresses (도로명주소) for taxi navigation, Seoul/Busan subway exit directions, and neighborhood etiquette notes.

Section 03

Terms of Use & Travel Disclaimer

All drama, film, and artist titles referenced on K-Spot Korea are the property of their respective studios, broadcasters, and creators, and are cited strictly for informational and geographic travel guide purposes. Store operating hours, admission fees, and local transit routes in South Korea may change seasonally; always double-check live operating hours in Naver Map or KakaoMap before departure. Many filming locations are located in private residential neighborhoods (such as Bukchon Hanok Village and Suwon Haenggung-dong)—please respect local residents' privacy and quiet hours.
